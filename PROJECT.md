# Project: The Armouries of the Third Deep (The One Ring 2e Adventure Module)

## Architecture
The adventure module is a masterclass 2–3 session tabletop RPG publication conforming strictly to *The One Ring 2nd Edition* (*Moria: Through the Doors of Durin*, Year 2989 TA). It is organized as a modular, highly readable handbook in `c:/Users/ante/Documents/Moria/adventures/armouries_of_the_third_deep/` with standalone play aids in `handouts/`.

## Interface Contracts
### Campaign Context (`01_campaign_context.md`) <-> Band Mechanics (`02_band_mechanics.md`)
- Torvir (Champion), Einar (Treasure Hunter), Khoril (Captain) profiles feed into Band leadership and disposition checks.
- 7 Companions (*Bláin, Fáin, Dúrmer, Hjoldring, Bróga, Austri, Dolg*) have defined base stats and health for the Band roster.

### Operational Mechanics (`03_operational_mechanics.md`) <-> Keyed Locations (`04_keyed_locations.md`)
- Every location references Alert Tracker levels (0–3) for patrol presence and ambient danger.
- Every location defines noise impact (+1 to +5) for loud actions (smashing doors, combat, horn).
- Toxic gas rooms (Rooms 7, 8) use the exact Miasma degradation rules and Craft/Healing TNs from 03_operational_mechanics.

### Adversaries (`05_adversaries_and_hazards.md`) <-> Keyed Locations (`04_keyed_locations.md`)
- Room 6 places The Mauler (AL 10) with exact terrain interactables (catwalks, stalactites, Riddle duel).
- Room 9 places Grimnar the Disgraced (AL 6) and ambush vanguard.
- Wandering patrols in Rooms 1, 3, 5 map to Udûn Sniffers and Orc Guards.

### Relics (`06_relics_and_rewards.md`) <-> Keyed Locations (`04_keyed_locations.md`)
- Room 7 contains the Dying Scribe's Letter prop clue.
- Room 8 contains the Tunnel-Guard wargear armaments.
- Room 9 requires the Marshal's Key + King's Key.
- Room 10 contains Durin's Axe (+4 Eye Awareness trigger).

### GM Playbook & Handouts (`07_gm_playbook_and_pacing.md` & `handouts/*`) <-> All Modules
- All room DCs, adversary stat summaries, and alert thresholds in `gm_cheat_sheet.md` and `band_worksheet.md` exactly mirror Chapters 1–6.
- `node_map.md` visualizes all connections, secret tunnels, and elevation changes from `04_keyed_locations.md`.
