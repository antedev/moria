## 1. Executive Summary & Setting Overview

This specification report documents the authoritative narrative, mechanical, and lore parameters for the forthcoming tabletop adventure module **"The Armouries of the Third Deep"**, set within Khazad-dûm during the historical reclamation attempt by **Balin son of Fundin** in **2989 TA**.

The expeditionary company consists of three distinguished Player-Heroes (**Torvir Hammerstone**, **Einar son of Anar**, and **Khoril Hornblower**) leading an armed vanguard of **6–8 veteran Dwarf Companions** (operating under *The One Ring 2e* Moria Band rules). Operating from their newly reclaimed and cleansed Safe Haven—**The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard**—the Company embarks on a high-stakes, 3-act subterranean infiltration into the ancient **Armouries of the Third Deep**.

### Core Campaign Parameters
- **System**: *The One Ring 2nd Edition* (Core Rules & *Moria: Through the Doors of Durin*).
- **Timeline**: 2989 TA (Early Balin's Expedition; ~8 delve sessions completed to date).
- **Staging Base**: *The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard* (cleansed Safe Haven on the Second Deep) and *The East-Gate / First Hall Encampment* (under Lord Balin & Commander Fróra).
- **Strategic Eye Awareness**: Baseline reset following the Extended Fellowship Phase at Dimrill Dale / East-Gate (Hunt Threshold 14–16).
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Infiltrate the Third Deep's sealed and poison-shrouded arsenals to recover serviceable Dwarf-forged wargear for Balin's growing garrison, and retrieve royal heirlooms (such as *Durin's Axe* or ancient tokens of kingship) to present to **King Dáin Ironfoot** in Erebor as undeniable proof to secure royal reinforcements.

## 4. Campaign Context & Staging

### 4.1 Historical Setting (Balin's Expedition 2989 TA)
* **The Strategic Situation**: In 2989 TA, Balin son of Fundin led a bold host of Longbeards from Erebor to reclaim Khazad-dûm. While early skirmishes cleared the East-Gate and established a foothold in the First Hall and Dimrill Dale, Balin's forces are dangerously small (under 100 fighting Dwarves) compared to the sprawling subterranean complex.
* **The East-Gate Encampment**: Located in the First Hall and the sheltered mountain folds of Dimrill Dale. Serves as the primary logistics hub, guarded by veteran Longbeards, pack-ponies, and stores of Beorning honey-rations and iron tools.
* **The High Council & Political Objective**:
  * **Lord Balin**: Passionate, noble, but carrying the heavy burden of leadership. Balin knows his colony cannot survive in isolation; they need royal sanction, Dwarven regiments, and master smiths from the Lonely Mountain.
  * **Commander Fróra (daughter of Dwalin)**: Balin's fiercely practical niece and field commander. She has tasked the Player-Heroes with recovering undeniable proof of Moria's vast usable wealth—specifically pristine ancient wargear and royal heirlooms from the legendary Third Deep—to convince King Dáin Ironfoot.
  * **King Dáin Ironfoot (in Erebor)**: Having looked into the shadow of the East-Gate after slaying Azog in 2799 TA, Dáin sensed the slumbering horror of Durin's Bane. He officially forbids royal military mobilization without irrefutable proof that Moria's great works and armouries can be reclaimed without awakening the doom of the Dwarves.

---

### 4.2 The Safe Haven: The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard
* **Location & Lore**: Located on the **Second Deep**, beneath the natural chasm of *Thrym's Cleft* (Mount Silvertine). Transformed in the First/Second Age into a magnificent subterranean garden of flowering stone, giant phosphorescent mushrooms, and healing herb beds.
* **Current Status**: Cleansed and Reclaimed as an official **Dwarven Safe Haven** during Milestone 5. The blood-drinking *Blood-vine* in the Tower of Thrym and spider broods have been eradicated.
* **Safe Haven Benefits**:
  * Allows the Fellowship to undertake *Fellowship Phase Undertakings* deeper underground.
  * Shifts travel routes between the First Hall and Thistlebeard from **Dark Land** (Hunt Threshold 14) to **Wild Land** (Hunt Threshold 16).
  * Grants fresh water, edible cave-mosses/fish, and safe lodging.
* **The Descent to the Third Deep**: Ancient architectural tomes retrieved from Thrym's tower library reveal forgotten vertical counterweight shafts, ventilation flues, and spiral stairs that lead directly from the lower grottoes of Thistlebeard's Caves down into the **Mustering-Yard and Upper Gatehouse of the Third Deep**.

---

### 4.3 Rescued Captives & Non-Combat NPCs (Stationed at East-Gate Camp)
1. **Frór**: A sullen, soot-stained Dwarf miner and brother to the traitorous *Bildor*. Deeply traumatized by his brother's descent into madness and betrayal. Frór works the forge at the East-Gate camp, warning the heroes that Bildor knows the secret crawlspaces of the Deeps.
2. **The Traumatized Dwarf**: A rescued survivor whose psyche shattered under Orcish captivity in the deep slave-mines. He lives in a perpetual waking dream, firmly convinced he is living in the glorious golden age of Khazad-dûm. He scratches remarkably accurate architectural diagrams of the Third Deep armouries on slate stones.
3. **Haldor**: A rugged Man of the Anduin Vale liberated from the Udûn slave pit. Grateful and steadfast, Haldor tends to camp fortifications and heavy baggage at Dimrill Dale.

## 5. Relics & Key Artifacts

### 5.1 The Eye of Thrym
* **Origin & Nature**: A carved stone pendant set with an azure sapphire, taken from the tomb of King Thrym Thistlebeard.
* **Attunement & Limitation**: The talisman is enchanted solely to resonate with the ancient sentinel statues throughout the *Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard*. When the wearer rests within the Caves, they can project their sight and hearing through any statue in the garden.
* **Current Status**: Carried by Torvir Hammerstone. **Completely inert and non-functional within the Third Deep** (does not grant scrying or remote vision outside Thistlebeard's Safe Haven).

---

### 5.2 The Broken Key
* **Origin & Nature**: A fractured, rune-carved Dwarven key of ancient make, salvaged from earlier delves.
* **Mechanical Function**: Grants a permanent **+2 modifier / Advantage (roll 2 Feat Dice, take the best)** on all **SCAN** tests made by the bearer.
* **Tactical Application in Delve**: Einar uses it to detect concealed trigger stones, hidden armory lockboxes, secret bypass tunnels in the Broken Hall, and weakened stone lintels.

---

### 5.3 Khoril's Battle-Horn of the Realm
* **Origin & Nature**: A massive silver-banded great horn of Khazad-dûm.
* **Mechanical Function**:
  * Grants **+1 to all Battle rolls** made by Khoril or the Band.
  * Allows Khoril to execute the *Rally Companions* tactical action in combat.
* **Acoustic Danger**: Blown within the enclosed vaulted chambers of the Third Deep, its booming resonance echoes across miles of stone, immediately advancing the **Alert Tracker by +1** and raising **Eye Awareness by +2**.

---

### 5.4 The Keys to the Royal Vault (The King's Door)
The sealed adamant portal to the Lower Armoury (Keyed Location 9: The King's Door) requires two synchronized master keys or an extraordinary bypass:
1. **The King's Key**: Canonical location documented in *The Last Redoubt of the Dwarves* (Fifth Hall), held in the skeletal hands of King Náin I on his Ring of Keys.
2. **The Marshal's Key**: Held by the ancient Marshal of the Armies who fell in the Sixteenth Hall of the Fifth Deep. Taken centuries ago by Goblins down to **Goblin Village** (Lower Deeps). The goblin informant **Grik the Skulker** knows its current possessor or whereabouts.
3. **Bypass Mechanics**: Can be bypassed via a Daunting (Resistance 9) Extended Craft/Enhearten Endeavour requiring specialized Dwarven tools (*Vaultbreaker* Gift, Einar's *Broken Key*, and masterwork smithing).

---

### 5.5 Durin's Axe (Royal Artifact Profile)
* **Type**: Legendary Great Axe (Wielded by Durin I and the Kings of Khazad-dûm).
* **Location**: Resting on the runic granite plinth in the center of the Lower Armoury (Keyed Location 10).
* **TOR 2e Enchanted Qualities**:
  * **Rune-scored**: Can never be broken, rusted, or blunted by magical or mundane forces.
  * **Superior Grievous**: Inflicts **+2 Damage** (Base Damage 9).
  * **Superior Keen**: Scores a Piercing Blow on a Feat Die roll of **8, 9, 10, or Gandalf Rune**.
  * **Flame of Hope**: When wielded by a Dwarf, spending 1 Hope inspires all allies in combat (+1d to attacks).
  * **Gleam of Terror**: Intimidate Foe combat tasks made by the wielder are Favoured; adversaries lose 2 Hate on a success.
* **The Weight of Doom**: Claiming Durin's Axe instantly raises the Fellowship's **Eye Awareness by +4**.

1. **The Mustering-Yard**: Vast subterranean crossroads hall marked by ancient Balrog scorches and colossal cracked pillars. Neutral ground between Moria and Udûn patrols.
2. **The Upper Gatehouse**: Crushed adamant blast-doors and structural collapse hazards; prime location for establishing a fortified fallback redoubt held by 2–3 Dwarf companions.
3. **The First Armoury**: Ransacked weapon racks, Orc tripwire scrap-traps, and discarded blades coated in black venom.
4. **The Broken Hall**: Desecrated Dwarven military museum; smashed murals of First/Second Age victories; crude Orc effigy/dark idol of Morgoth/Balrog triggering Dread tests (unique psychological hooks for Torvir's vengeance and Einar's greed).
5. **The Second Armoury**: Heavy siege machinery, counterweight iron catapults, and steel-headed battering rams (usable for barricading or setting traps).
6. **The Hall of the Mauler**: Enormous vaulted drill chamber littered with weapon piles, high catwalks, and loose ceiling stalactites; lair of the heavily armoured Great Cave-troll.
7. **The Poisoned Halls (12th & 14th Halls)**: Council chambers of ancient Dwarven captains; bodies of fallen knights preserved in stasis by the lingering Balrog miasma. Contains the dying scribe's letter detailing the lost Marshal's Key.
8. **The Upper Armoury**: Poison-filled storage bays containing hundreds of serviceable Dwarf-forged mail-shirts, helms, and polearms for Balin's soldiers.
9. **The King's Door**: Massive portal emblazoned with Durin's Crown and Anvil, sealed with ancient locking mechanisms requiring the King's Key and Marshal's Key (or a masterwork Craft bypass).
10. **The Lower Armoury (Royal Vault)**: Pristine royal treasury containing the Greater Hoard, legendary gromril wargear, and **Durin's Axe**.

### 7.2 Tactical Alert Subsystem & Eye Awareness

To prevent immediate overwhelming swarms while maintaining subterranean tension, delve actions interact with a **4-Stage Alert Tracker**:

| Alert Level | Designation | Dungeon Condition & Enemy Behavior |
|:---:|---|---|
| **Alert 0** | *Quiet Shadows* | Standard dungeon patrol routines; Orcs unaware of intruders. Stealth rolls standard. |
| **Alert 1** | *Unease & Scent* | Cave bats and Udûn sniffers dispatched to check unusual sounds/drafts. Random encounters on $\oint$. |
| **Alert 2** | *Hunted & Barricaded* | Orc guards fortify major corridors; **Grimnar the Disgraced** actively stalks the squad, preparing ambushes. |
| **Alert 3** | *Drums in the Deep* | General alarm sounded. Elite shock-troops mobilize from Malech's fortress. A countdown begins before the Upper Gatehouse shafts are sealed. |

#### Sound Economy Table
* Silent Takedown / Stealth kill within 1 round: +0 Alert.
* Combat lasting 2–3 rounds: +1 Alert.
* Combat lasting 4+ rounds: +2 Alert, +1 Eye Awareness.
* Blown Battle-Horn / Crashing Siege Engines: +1 Alert, +2 Eye Awareness.
* Breaking Stone / Triggered Collapse: +1 Alert, +1 Eye Awareness.

### 7.3 2–3 Session Delve Structure

* **Session 1: Act I — The Descent & Securing the Foothold**
  * Journey down the vertical shafts from Thistlebeard's Safe Haven.
  * Infiltration of the Mustering-Yard; encounter with Grik the Skulker.
  * Securing and fortifying the Upper Gatehouse as a tactical base of operations (deploying rearguard companions).
* **Session 2: Act II — The Despoiled Halls & The Poisoned Vaults**
  * Exploration of the Broken Hall and Second Armoury; dismantling Orc traps.
  * Braving the toxic Balrog gas in the Twelfth and Fourteenth Halls using herbal remedies/protective masks.
  * Recovering the dying scribe's letter and securing the Upper Armoury wargear.
* **Session 3: Act III — The Clash with the Mauler, The Royal Vault & Fighting Withdrawal**
  * Tactical engagement or riddle-duel bypass of the Mauler.
  * Grimnar's ambush at the King's Door.
  * Unlocking the King's Door and claiming *Durin's Axe*.
  * High-tension fighting withdrawal back up through the Upper Gatehouse while the Band holds the choke points against garrison alarms.
