## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Mapping Locations to Narrative Nodes**:
   - Locations 1, 2, 3 naturally form **Act I: The Descent & Forward Staging**. The Mustering-Yard introduces the scale and sentries; the Upper Gatehouse provides a critical strategic decision to leave a rearguard and fortify the retreat route; the First Armoury establishes the threat of Orc venom and traps.
   - Locations 4, 5, 7, 8 form **Act II: Despoiled Halls & Toxic Hazard**. The Broken Hall delivers deep psychological Dread (Morgoth Idol) and lore clues; the Second Armoury provides a heavy siege-engine sandbox (Grond-ram, torsion ballista); the Poisoned Halls introduce the Balrog miasma hazard and the dying scribe's letter clue to the Marshal's Key; the Upper Armoury provides the military wargear necessary to equip Balin's vanguard.
   - Locations 6, 9, 10 form **Act III: The Apex Encounter, Royal Vault & Fighting Withdrawal**. The Hall of the Mauler provides an arena boss fight (catwalks, stalactites, Riddle duel); the King's Door tests lockpicking/key acquisition under the pressure of Grimnar's ambush; the Lower Armoury rewards the party with *Durin's Axe* and enchanted relics.
   - The climactic trigger upon claiming *Durin's Axe* (+4 Eye Awareness / alerting the entire deep) immediately initiates the **Fighting Withdrawal** across Locations 6 → 5 → 4 → 2 → 1, where earlier fortifications at Location 2 (Gatehouse) and companion deployment directly determine survival.

2. **Integrating Band Operations**:
   - To make the 7 Dwarf Companions mechanically vital without bogging down initiative, four distinct tactical deployment roles were established: *Forward Scout Screen* (2 Dwarves, rolling Band Vigilance), *Rearguard Choke Defense* (2–3 Dwarves at Location 2), *Heavy Salvage Porter Squad* (4 Dwarves hauling mail-shirts from Location 8), and *Shield-Wall Phalanx* (4 Dwarves in Defensive Stance intercepting attacks).

3. **Operational Balance via Alert Economy**:
   - Standard D&D-style alarms cause instant TPKs in Moria. A 4-stage tiered system (0: Quiet Shadows, 1: Unease & Scent, 2: Hunted & Barricaded, 3: Drums in the Deep) driven by defined Noise Points (0 for stealth, +1 for quiet melee, +2 for loud melee, +3 for stone smashing, +4 for siege weapons, +5 for Khoril's battle horn) gives the players agency to manage risk dynamically.

4. **Facilitator Tool Suite & Modular Architecture**:
   - To maximize table usability for the GM, the module is broken into 8 core markdown chapters plus a dedicated `handouts/` folder containing the 1-Page Rapid Cheat Sheet, Band Worksheet, ASCII Node Maps, and the in-world prop *Dying Scribe's Letter*.
