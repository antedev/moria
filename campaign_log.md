## 3. Major NPCs & Faction Dynamics

### The Free Peoples / Dwarven Expedition
* **Balin, son of Fundin (Patron & Lord of Moria)**: Has now arrived in person at the East-Gate camp with his vanguard. Passionate and determined to reclaim Khazad-dûm, but acutely aware that his forces are too few to hold multiple halls and deeps without royal backing from Erebor.
* **Fróra, daughter of Dwalin (Camp Commander)**: Balin's niece. Established the advance camp in Dimrill Dale / East-Gate. Desperately seeking tangible evidence and ancient relics from the delve to persuade King Dáin Ironfoot to grant royal approval and dispatch major Dwarven armies and supplies.
* **King Dáin Ironfoot (In Erebor)**: Refuses to sanction a full reclamation of Moria due to the lingering dread of Durin's Bane. Demands undeniable proof before committing the lives and wealth of the Longbeards.

### The Minions of Shadow & Under-dwellers
* **Udûn Orcs (The Deep Slavers)**:
  * Ruled from the volcanic Deeps; operating deep mining prisons ("Shadow-mines").
  * Their slave mine was raided by the PCs, prisoners were liberated, and their captive goblin was slain.
  * An aggressive, industrial orcish faction likely to send trackers to reclaim lost stock.
* **Grimnar (Disgraced Orc Chieftain)**:
  * Formerly stationed at the *Ledge of Woe*; took over the defence of Durin's Bridge after the fall of *Gorklakh*.
  * Defeated at the Bridge by the PCs; fled into the depths. Currently disgraced in the eyes of Great Orc Lord Malech and lurking in the shadows seeking vengeance.
* **Malech (The Great Orc / Lord of the Dwarrowdelf)**:
  * Controls the *Fortress of Malech / Palace of Stairs*.
  * Connected directly to Thrym Thistlebeard's Caves via a remodeled mine cart track (which the party discovered but has not yet ridden). Malech does not yet know the Dwarves hold Thistlebeard's redoubt.
* **Granny Goblin & Grik (The Goblin Underground)**:
  * Grik, an emissary for Granny Goblin, warned the PCs about the Udûn Orcs and sought to manipulate the Dwarves into slaughtering the Mordor Orcs.
  * Grik claimed to know who holds the key to the *Pillar Door on the Stairs*, but kept the secret for future leverage.
* **Bildor (Renamed from Buna in book)**:
  * Seduced by the Shadow; pretended to search for his brother Frór, but intended to sacrifice the PCs to the Udûn Orcs.
  * Knocked unconscious during the prison raid. Left behind by the PCs who believed him dead. **He survived**, and remains an active wild card.

## 4. Reclaimed Relics & Key Lore

1. **The Eye of Thrym**: A mysterious stone artifact enabling the bearer to scry through the ancient statues scattered throughout Thistlebeard's Caves. (Carried by Torvir).
2. **The Broken Key**: A wondrous Dwarven key that grants a blessing (+2 / Advantage) to **Scan** rolls. (Carried by Einar).
3. **The Ancient Elven Necklace of Beleriand**: A First Age relic granting blessings to both **Insight** and **Awe** (inflicted Shadow upon being retrieved).
4. **The Pillar Door on the Stairs & The Missing Key**:
   * Located along the *Dimrill Stair / Mountain Galleries*.
   * The party found physical traces that Mordor Orcs hold/held the key.
   * Grik knows the exact whereabouts/bearer of the key.
5. **The Secret Route to the Third Deep**: Lore books recovered from Thrym Thistlebeard’s tower map out the descending shafts down into the **Armouries of the Third Deep**.
6. **Known Distant Landmarks**: Rumours heard of the **Ledge of Woe** (Deep 4/5) and the **Fifth Hall (The Last Redoubt of the Dwarves)**.
