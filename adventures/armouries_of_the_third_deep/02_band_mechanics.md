## 1. The Moria Band System: Core Rules & Foundations

In *The One Ring 2e* supplement *Moria: Through the Doors of Durin*, expeditions operating in deep subterranean territory utilize the **Band Mechanics** to manage supporting companions, tactical formations, mass skirmishes, and collective endurance without bogging down individual initiative.The expeditionary force commands a seasoned vanguard of **seven veteran Dwarf Companions** (*Bláin, Fáin, Dúrmer, Hjoldring, Bróga, Austri, Dolg*).

```
========================================================================================
                                BAND STATISTICAL PROFILE
========================================================================================
 BAND READINESS RATING:       5 (Base 4 + 1 for Hardened Veteran Dúrmer)
 BAND READINESS TN:          15 (Calculated as 20 - Band Readiness)
 ACTIVE BAND SIZE:            Medium Band (7 Active Veteran Companions)
 STARTING BAND BURDEN:        Medium Burden (Prepared Ingress) -> Heavy (When Salvaging)
 STARTING BAND HOPE:         12 Points (Restored from Fellowship Pool)
 STARTING BAND SHADOW:        1 Point (Lingering trauma from prior delves)
 FELLOWSHIP POOL:             4 Points
========================================================================================
```

### 1.1 Band Readiness & Target Number (TN)
A Band’s efficiency, discipline, and collective grit are represented by its **Band Readiness Rating**.

$$\mathbf{\text{Band Readiness TN}} = 20 - \text{Band Readiness} = 20 - 5 = \mathbf{15}$$

* **Making Band Tests**: When the Band executes a collective tactical action, the GM calls for a test using one of the five **Band Dispositions**. The roll consists of **1 Feat Die + Success Dice equal to the Disposition Rating**. If the total equals or exceeds the **Readiness TN (15)** (modified by enemy Might or environmental threats), the action succeeds.
* **Success Icons ($\mathbf{6s}$)**: Each Success icon rolled on a Band test allows the Band to trigger specialized squad maneuvers (e.g. flanking, disarming traps silently, or shielding an ally).
* **Readiness Degradation**: If the Band suffers catastrophic losses (e.g. 3 or more companions slain or lost), the Readiness Rating drops from 5 to 4 ($\text{TN}$ rises to 16).

---

### 1.2 The Five Band Dispositions

The Band’s tactical capabilities are divided into five specialized ratings:

```
                          ┌───────────────────────────┐
                          │   BAND READINESS: 5       │
                          │   (READINESS TN: 15)      │
                          └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                                        │
     ┌──────────────┬──────────────┬────┴─────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
     ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
  [WAR: 3]     [VIGILANCE: 2] [MANOEUVRE: 2] [EXPERTISE: 2]  [RALLY: 1]   [HOPE: 12]
 (Combat,       (Scouting,     (Silent March, (Engineering,  (Morale,     (Shared Pool)
  Phalanx,       Perimeter,     Withdrawal,    Locks,         Fatigue,
  Breaching)     Ambushes)      Evasion)       First Aid)     Courage)
```

1. **WAR (Rating 3 — 3d6)**:
   * *Scope*: Resolving combat clashes, executing shield-wall charges, breaching reinforced barricades, and holding choke points.
   * *Specialist Champions*: Dúrmer (*Mighty*), Bláin (*Goblin-Slayer*), Dolg (*Shield-Bearer*).
2. **VIGILANCE (Rating 2 — 2d6)**:
   * *Scope*: Scouting vaulted chambers, setting perimeter sentry watches, spotting Orc ambushes, and detecting acoustic echoes.
   * *Specialist Champions*: Austri (*Scout*), Fáin (*Dead-Eye*).
3. **MANOEUVRE (Rating 2 — 2d6)**:
   * *Scope*: Silent marching through echo chambers, navigating collapsed stairwells, rapid tactical redeployment, and disciplined fighting withdrawals.
   * *Specialist Champions*: Austri (*Scout*), Bróga (*Vaultbreaker*).
4. **EXPERTISE (Rating 2 — 2d6)**:
   * *Scope*: Crafting alchemical respirators, disarming complex mechanical traps, forcing ancient locks, repairing damaged wargear, and assessing stone masonry.
   * *Specialist Champions*: Hjoldring (*Smith*), Bróga (*Vaultbreaker*).
5. **RALLY (Rating 1 — 1d6)**:
   * *Scope*: Resisting the supernatural Dread of Morgoth/Balrog idols, making Band Fatigue tests, recovering from shock, and performing emergency triage under fire.
   * *Specialist Champions*: Supported directly by a hero's *Leadership* Trait or the *Battle-horn of the Realm*.

## 2. The Four Tactical Squad Deployments

The Player-Heroes can assign their seven companions to four distinct tactical roles depending on the operational needs of the delve. Assigning companions to these roles modifies group tests and provides specific tactical perks.

```
========================================================================================
                               TACTICAL SQUAD DEPLOYMENTS
========================================================================================
 ROLE                      ASSIGNED DWARVES    PRIMARY BENEFIT & MECHANIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Forward Scout Screen   Austri & Bróga      Rolls Vigilance (2d) vs Band TN 15. Prevents 
                                               surprise; enables Favoured SCAN rolls for 
                                               heroes inspecting the route.
 2. Shield-Wall Phalanx    Dúrmer, Dolg,       Rolls War (3d) vs Band TN 15. Intercepts 1 
                           Bláin               melee attack/round; prevents swarming.
 3. Rearguard Choke Point  Bláin & Fáin        Held at Location 2 Gatehouse. Guarantees 
                                               escape line; grants +1d on withdrawal tests.
 4. Heavy Salvage Porters  Hjoldring & Dúrmer  Hauls 40+ suits of mail from Location 8.
                                               Shifts Burden to Heavy; equips vanguard.
========================================================================================
```

---

### 2.1 Role 1: Forward Scout Screen
* **Assigned Roster**: **Austri the Wayfinder** & **Bróga Vaultbreaker** (2 Dwarves).
* **Positioning**: Advances 40–50 paces ahead of the main body, hugging shadow lines, archway cornices, and drainage troughs.
* **Operational Rules**:
  * Whenever the expedition enters a new keyed location or intersection, the GM rolls **Band Vigilance (2d6)** or **Band Manoeuvre (2d6)** against **Band TN 15** (modified by ambient Alert Level).
  * **On a Success**: The scouts identify enemy sentry posts (such as the Udûn sniffers in Location 1), tripwires, or acoustic traps before the main expedition steps into danger. The Company cannot be surprised, and Player-Heroes gain **+1d** on their opening actions.
  * **Scouting Synergy**: A hero moving alongside the screen can make **SCAN rolls (Favoured)** to locate hidden compartments or mechanisms safely without breaking group stealth.
  * **On a Failure**: The scouts blunder into an acoustic tripwire or loose shale, generating **+1 Noise Point** on the Alert Tracker.

---

### 2.2 Role 2: Shield-Wall Phalanx
* **Assigned Roster**: **Dúrmer the Iron-Shouldered**, **Dolg the Bulwark**, and **Bláin the Cleaver** (3 Dwarves).
* **Positioning**: Formed in the central or front ranks, locking broad rim-reinforced shields across narrow corridors (10–15 feet wide) or doorframes.
* **Operational Rules**:
  * **Interception Reaction**: Once per combat round, Dolg or Dúrmer can interpose their tower-shields to absorb one incoming physical melee attack targeted at a Player-Hero or wounded companion. The damage is resolved against the companion’s Endurance and Armour dice (Armour 3d/4d).
  * **Anti-Swarm Bastion**: While the Shield-Wall remains unbroken, enemy adversaries cannot utilize flanking bonuses, and Orc swarm attacks (such as Udûn Sniffers trying to overwhelm through numbers) cannot target rear-rank characters (such as Fáin or archers in Rearward stance).
  * **Shield-Bash Manoeuvre**: When the Band tests **War (3d6)** in combat, spending a Success icon ($\mathbf{6}$) allows the Phalanx to push the enemy frontline back 10 paces, creating room for devastating close combat strikes.

---

### 2.3 Role 3: Rearguard Choke Point Defense
* **Assigned Roster**: **Bláin the Cleaver** and **Fáin Iron-Visor** (2 Dwarves).
* **Positioning**: Stationed at a fortified fallback position, specifically **Keyed Location 2: The Upper Gatehouse**.
* **Operational Rules**:
  * **Securing the Line of Retreat**: Leaving 2 companions to fortify and hold Location 2 creates an active forward redoubt. The rearguard watches the murder-holes, keeps the barricades secure, and prevents enemy patrols from cutting off the transverse retreat gallery (Westward Drift) back to Thrym's Caves.
  * **Extraction Bonus**: During Act III's *Fighting Withdrawal*, having an established Rearguard provides **+1d** on Band extraction tests and grants the Company one automatic free round to cross the Mustering-Yard without taking missile fire.
  * **Keystone Trap Trigger**: Fáin and Bláin can be instructed to pull the ceiling winch keystone, triggering a controlled cave-in on pursuing Orc warbands (dealing 30 damage / crushing the pursuing vanguard).

---

### 2.4 Role 4: Heavy Salvage Porter Squad
* **Assigned Roster**: **Hjoldring the Armourer** and **Dúrmer the Iron-Shouldered** (assisted by 2 additional companions if available).
* **Positioning**: Formed in the center of the column with heavy pack litters, hemp ropes, and padded leather panniers.
* **Operational Rules**:
  * **Salvage Operation**: In **Location 8 (The Upper Armoury)**, the squad can pack up to 40 suits of ancient Dwarf mail-shirts, 30 shields, and crates of masterwork weapons.
  * **Burden Shift**: Packing this immense weight shifts the Band’s Burden rating from **Medium Burden** to **Heavy / Overburdened**:
    * Band Manoeuvre rolls suffer **-1d**.
    * Marching Fatigue tests suffer a **-1d penalty** (or **-2d** if fully Overburdened).
    * Stealth checks to move silently generate **+1 Noise Point** per hall traversed unless padded with cloth (**CRAFT roll**).
  * **Strategic Payoff**: Safely extracting this wargear back to the East-Gate Camp equips 40–50 frontline Dwarves in Balin’s vanguard with gromril-mail and masterwork weapons, eliminating the colony's defensive vulnerability and securing royal recognition from King Dáin.

## 3. Band Condition, Morale & Injury Systems

Subterranean warfare in Khazad-dûm exacts a brutal toll on living flesh and dwarven resolve. The Band tracks four interlocking operational metrics: **Band Hope**, **Band Shadow**, **Band Injury**, and **Band Fatigue**.

```
========================================================================================
                               BAND HEALTH & MORALE DASHBOARD
========================================================================================
 BAND HOPE POOL:      [ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ■ ] (12 / 12 Max Points)
 BAND SHADOW:         [ ■ □ □ □ □ □ □ □ □ □ □ □ ] (1 Point - Cleansed baseline)
 WEARY CONDITION:     TRIGGERED WHEN >= 4 COMPANIONS INJURED (SEVERE+) OR SPENT
 DESPERATE STAND:     HEROIC SACRIFICE ON FAILED CRITICAL TEST (G RUNE TO SURVIVE)
========================================================================================
```

---

### 3.1 Band Hope & Shadow Dynamics

* **Band Hope (Starting Pool: 12 Points)**:
  * Band Hope represents the collective courage, camaraderie, and divine resilience of Durin's Folk.
  * *Expending Hope*: A Player-Hero can spend 1 point of Band Hope to grant **+1d** on any Band Disposition roll (War, Vigilance, Manoeuvre, Expertise, Rally) or to activate a companion’s Gift in a critical moment.
  * *Restoring Hope*: Band Hope is restored by resting in a Safe Haven (Thrym's Caves), by Khoril playing his *Battle-horn of the Realm*, or by achieving major milestones (such as toppling the Balrog Idol in Location 4 or claiming Durin's Axe).
* **Band Shadow (Starting Rating: 1 Point)**:
  * Moria is an ancient wound in the earth, heavy with the spiritual malice of the Shadow.
  * *Accumulating Shadow*:
    * An ally suffers a **Severe or Grievous Injury** $\rightarrow \mathbf{+1\text{ Shadow Point (Dread)}}$.
    * An ally is **slain, lost, or sacrificed** $\rightarrow \mathbf{+2\text{ Shadow Points (Dread)}}$.
    * The Company witnesses an ancient desecration (e.g. the idol in Location 4) $\rightarrow$ Party rolls Dread tests; failure adds Shadow.
  * *Shadow Breakdown*: If Band Shadow ever exceeds Band Hope, the Band becomes **Miserable**. In this state, any roll showing the Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$) on the Feat die results in an automatic catastrophic failure and panic.

---

### 3.2 Band Injury System (The 5 Tiers)

When the Band is targeted by area attacks, traps, or melee clashes, injuries are resolved against individual companion profiles according to five formal severity tiers:

```
[FLEETING] ──(Prolonged Rest)──> [MODERATE] ──(First Aid)──> [SEVERE] ──(Emergency 1-Hr)──> [GRIEVOUS]
(Brief Rest)                     (Prolonged)                  (+1 Shadow)                    (Dying!)
```

```
========================================================================================
                               THE FIVE INJURY TIERS
========================================================================================
 TIER         SEVERITY & EFFECTS                       RECOVERY & TREATMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Fleeting   Bruises, shallow cuts, winded (-2 End)   Cleared completely after a Brief 
                                                       Rest (30 minutes).
 2. Moderate   Deep gashes, cracked ribs (-5 End)       Cleared after a Prolonged Rest in 
                                                       a Safe Haven (Thrym's Caves).
 3. Severe     Broken bones, deep thrusts (-10 End).    Requires First Aid (**HEALING roll** 
               Adds +1 Band Shadow (Dread).             or Band EXPERTISE 2d vs Band TN 15) 
                                                       during Prolonged Rest to reduce to 
                                                       Moderate.
 4. Grievous   DYING! Mortal wound, artery severed.     MUST receive First Aid (**HEALING roll** 
               Adds +1 Band Shadow (Dread).             or Band EXPERTISE vs Band TN 15) within 
                                                       1 hour or companion dies. Prolonged 
                                                       rest reduces to Lingering.
 5. Lingering  Permanent crippling trauma. Second       Permanent until next Fellowship Phase. 
               Grievous wound causes INSTANT DEATH.     -1d on all individual actions.
========================================================================================
```

* **Endurance Test vs Damage Threat**: When a companion is struck by a trap or weapon blow, the GM rolls Band **RALLY** (1d6) against **Band TN 15 + Threat Modifier**:
  * *Bothersome Threat (+0)*: Arrow grazing, stone chip $\rightarrow$ Fleeting Injury.
  * *Painful Threat (+1)*: Orc scimitar strike, spear thrust $\rightarrow$ Moderate Injury.
  * *Vicious Threat (+2)*: Troll club, falling stalactite $\rightarrow$ Severe Injury.
  * *Dreadful Threat (+3)*: Cave-in crushing blow, Balrog fire $\rightarrow$ Grievous Injury.

---

### 3.3 Band Fatigue System (The 4 Tiers)

Marching through suffocating, airless shafts while carrying tons of wargear drains physical stamina. Fatigue progresses across four tiers:

$$\mathbf{\text{Fatigued}} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Faltering}} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Spent}} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Collapsed}}$$

* **Fatigue Testing**: After every 4 hours of heavy subterranean travel, or after navigating toxic zones (Location 7), the GM rolls Band **RALLY** (1d6) against **Band TN 15 + Fatigue Points**, modified by Band Burden:
  * *Light Burden*: **+1d** on the test.
  * *Medium Burden (Standard)*: Standard roll (1d6).
  * *Heavy Burden (Salvage Mode)*: **-1d** on the test.
  * *Overburdened*: **-2d** on the test.
* **Fatigue Effects**:
  * *Fatigued*: Companions complain of aching limbs; no mechanical penalty.
  * *Faltering*: Band Hope cannot be spent on Rally rolls.
  * *Spent*: Band Readiness drops by 1 ($\text{TN 16}$).
  * *Collapsed*: Companions must be carried on litters; travel speed halved; counts as incapacitated.

---

### 3.4 The Band Weary Condition

If **50% or more (4 or more)** of the seven companions are incapacitated—meaning they are suffering from **Severe Injury, Grievous Injury, Spent Fatigue, or Collapsed status**—the entire Band becomes **Band Weary**.

$$\mathbf{\text{BAND WEARY EFFECT:}}\quad \text{All Success dice rolled on Band Disposition tests showing } \mathbf{1, 2, \text{ or } 3} \mathbf{\text{ count as } 0.}$$

* *Restoring Freshness*: The Weary condition is removed only when enough companions receive First Aid, hot food, and rest to bring the number of incapacitated allies below 4.

---

### 3.5 The Desperate Stand Mechanic

When a Player-Hero or companion is about to suffer a mortal blow, or when a critical Band test fails during a high-stakes escape, an uninjured or lightly injured companion can choose to make a **Desperate Stand** (self-sacrifice).

```
========================================================================================
                              DESPERATE STAND RESOLUTION
========================================================================================
 1. TRIGGER: A Player-Hero suffers a lethal wound, or a critical Band Clash fails.
 2. DECLARATION: A companion (e.g. Dolg or Dúrmer) throws themselves into the breach.
 3. RE-ROLL: The failed test is immediately re-rolled FAVOURED (roll 2 Feat dice, take best) 
    and INSPIRED (Success dice are doubled).
 4. SURVIVAL CHECK (THE FEAT DIE):
    • GANDALF RUNE (G): The companion miraculously survives the impossible feat, 
      suffering only a Moderate Injury!
    • ANY OTHER RESULT (1-10 or Eye): The companion is SLAIN or LOST in the dark. 
      The Company immediately gains +2 BAND SHADOW (Dread).
========================================================================================
```

## 4. Stealth & Marching Discipline

Navigating the echoing stone vaults of Khazad-dûm requires rigid marching discipline. The acoustics of Moria amplify the slightest scraping of mail or clinking of belt buckles.

```
========================================================================================
                             TACTICAL MARCHING FORMATIONS
========================================================================================
 FORMATION             CONFIGURATION                  OPERATIONAL BENEFIT / DRAWBACK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 A. Ingress Column     Single file; scouts 40 paces   Standard speed; +1d to detect traps; 
                       ahead; vanguard leads.         Vulnerable to broadside ambushes.
 B. Shadow Crawl       Felt-padded boots; muffled     Movement speed halved; all Stealth 
                       mail; lantern shuttered.       rolls gain +1d; -1 Noise on all actions.
 C. Phalanx Advance    Locked shields in front/rear;  Immune to surprise charges; movement 
                       weapons drawn.                 speed slow; generates +1 Noise.
 D. Withdrawal Column  Rearguard holding choke;       Maximum movement speed; +2 to escape 
                       porters in center.             clashes; rear rank exposed to arrows.
========================================================================================
```

---

### 4.1 Group Stealth & Marching Tests
* **Resolving Group Movement**: When the expedition attempts to traverse hostile sectors or bypass enemy sentry posts (such as the Udûn sniffers in Location 1 or wandering patrols in Location 5), marching discipline is tested:
  * **Leader Check**: The company's Guide or leader makes a **TRAVEL roll** or **ENHEARTEN roll** (invoking a Trait such as *Leadership* for **+1d**).
  * **Alternative Band Check**: The GM rolls Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d6) against **Band TN 15** ($20 - \text{Readiness } 5$).
* **Noise Escalation & Consequences of Failure**:
  * **Failure**: Clanking wargear or loose gravel echoes down the galleries: **+1 Alert / Noise Point**.
  * **Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$)**: A dropped pry-bar or slipped shield clatters on stone (**+2 Alert / Noise Points**), immediately triggering a wandering scout patrol check.
* **Degrees of Success & Noise Reduction on $\mathbf{6}$s**:
  * **Success**: The squad moves silently in the shadows of the pillars: **+0 Noise Points** generated.
  * **Success Icons ($\mathbf{6}$)**: Each $\mathbf{6}$ rolled reduces ambient suspicion by **-1 Noise Point** (stepping in rhythm to muffle gear), or grants **+1d** to the Point Scout's next check.
  * **Gandalf Rune ($\mathbf{G}$)**: Flawless stealth; the squad discovers a forgotten maintenance flue, completely bypassing the next encounter zone.

## 5. Mass Combat: Battles & Clashes

When the expedition is engaged by organized Orc warbands (such as Grimnar's stalkers or garrison reinforcements), combat can be resolved using the **Band Clash** subsystem.

```
========================================================================================
                             BAND CLASH RESOLUTION FLOW
========================================================================================
  STEP 1: CHOOSE BAND STANCE (Aggressive / Balanced / Guarded / Fleeing)
                              │
                              ▼
  STEP 2: HERO LEADER ACTION (Command / Inspire / Fight / Duel)
                              │
                              ▼
  STEP 3: RESOLVE CLASH ROLL (Band War or Manoeuvre vs Band TN 15 + Foe Might)
                              │
                              ▼
  STEP 4: DAMAGE & RESISTANCE (Reduce Enemy Resistance; resolve companion casualties)
========================================================================================
```

---

### 5.1 Step 1: Band Stances
At the start of each combat round, the Company chooses a collective Band Stance:

1. **Aggressive Stance**:
   * *Effect*: The Band launches a ferocious assault. The Clash roll is **Ill-favoured**, but on any success, it automatically reduces enemy Resistance by an additional **1 point**.
2. **Balanced Stance**:
   * *Effect*: Standard operational stance. Full tactical flexibility; roll standard dice pools.
3. **Guarded Stance**:
   * *Effect*: The Band locks shields and focuses on survival. The Clash roll is **Favoured**, and the Band ignores the first Special Damage trigger rolled by the enemy.
4. **Fleeing Stance**:
   * *Effect*: The Band attempts to disengage. Roll Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d6) vs **Band TN 15 + Enemy Might**. Success allows the squad to retreat to the previous keyed location.

---

### 5.2 Step 2: Hero Leader Actions
During a Clash round, each Player-Hero takes a specialized Leader Action before the Band rolls:

* **Command**: A hero makes a **BATTLE roll** (invoking a Trait such as *Leadership* for **+1d**). On a success, the hero grants **+1d** to the Band’s Clash roll and can change the Band’s Stance mid-round.
* **Inspire**: A hero makes an **ENHEARTEN roll**. Success restores **1 point of Band Hope** or clears the *Faltering* fatigue condition from all companions.
* **Fight**: A hero attacks the enemy frontline in a close combat stance using their Combat Proficiency against the adversary's Parry rating. A successful attack reduces enemy Resistance by the weapon’s full Damage.
* **Duel**: A hero engages the enemy Champion (e.g. Grimnar the Disgraced) in single combat, nullifying the Champion's Might penalty on the Band Clash roll while locked in duel.

---

### 5.3 Step 3: Clash Roll & Enemy Resistance
* **The Clash Roll**: The GM rolls Band **WAR** (3d6) against $\mathbf{\text{Band TN 15}} + \text{Enemy Might}$.
* **Enemy War Party Profiles**:
  * **Patrol (3–4 Orcs)**: Might 0 | Resistance 3 | Threat: Bothersome (+0)
  * **Pack (6–8 Orcs)**: Might 1 | Resistance 6 | Threat: Painful (+1)
  * **Warband (12–16 Orcs)**: Might 2 | Resistance 9 | Threat: Vicious (+2)
  * **Horde (20+ Orcs / Black Uruks)**: Might 3 | Resistance 12 | Threat: Dreadful (+3)
* **Resolving Casualties**:
  * Each point of damage dealt by the Heroes and the Band reduces the Enemy Resistance pool. When Resistance reaches 0, the enemy breaks and flees.
  * If the Band fails the Clash roll, the enemy inflicts an **Injury Test** on 1d3 companions.
