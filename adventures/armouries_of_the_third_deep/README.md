# The Armouries of the Third Deep — Adventure Module Suite
## A Masterclass Tabletop Delve for *The One Ring 2e* (*Moria: Through the Doors of Durin*)

---

Welcome to the complete adventure module for **The Armouries of the Third Deep**, designed specifically for **Torvir Hammerstone** (*Champion*, STR 13/HRT 18/WIT 15), **Einar son of Anar** (*Treasure Hunter*, STR 14/HRT 17/WIT 15), and **Khoril Hornblower** (*Captain*, STR 13/HRT 16/WIT 16) leading their squad of **7 Dwarf Companions** (Band Readiness 5, **Band TN 15**) during the opening era of **Balin's Expedition (2989 T.A.)**.
