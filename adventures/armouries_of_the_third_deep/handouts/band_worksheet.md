## 5. The Desperate Stand Resolution Flowchart

When a Player-Hero or companion faces a lethal blow or when a critical escape roll fails:

```
========================================================================================================================
                                      DESPERATE STAND QUICK GUIDE
========================================================================================================================
 1. DECLARATION   : A companion (e.g. Dolg or Dúrmer) heroically throws themselves into the breach.
 2. RE-ROLL       : The failed test is immediately re-rolled FAVOURED (roll 2 Feat dice, take best) 
                    and INSPIRED (Success dice total is doubled!).
 3. SURVIVAL CHECK: Examine the Feat Die of the re-roll:
    • GANDALF RUNE (G) : The companion miraculously survives, suffering only a Moderate Injury!
    • ANY OTHER RESULT : The companion is SLAIN or LOST in the dark.
                         The Company immediately gains +2 BAND SHADOW (Dread).
========================================================================================================================
```
