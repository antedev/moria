# Handout: The Dying Scribe's Slate

> *"To whoever walks these halls in the light of later days: let not the heritage of Durin die in the suffocating dark."*  
> — *Frár son of Frerin, Scribe of the Third Deep (Anno 1981 TA)*

---

## 1. Physical Description & Table Prop Notes

```
========================================================================================
                                 PROP SPECIFICATION
========================================================================================
 OBJECT:         A slab of dark grey basalt slate, roughly 14 inches long and 9 inches 
                 wide, fractured diagonally across its lower left corner.
 INSCRIPTION:    Etched with an iron-tipped chisel in formal Angerthas Moria (Cirth Runes), 
                 hurriedly carved into the stone with trembling strokes.
 PHYSICAL CUES:  The grooves of the runes are filled with dried, blackened Dwarf-blood and 
                 a crust of sulfurous yellow soot from the Balrog's toxic miasma.
 LOCATION FOUND: Clutched in the petrified, mail-clad skeletal hands of Scribe Frár, seated 
                 against the council dais in Keyed Location 7 (The Poisoned Halls).
========================================================================================
```

## 5. GM Table Facilitation & Narrative Cues

### When to Present This Handout
* **Location**: Present this handout when the Company explores **Keyed Location 7: The Poisoned Halls** and searches the council dais where Scribe Frár's body lies preserved in stasis.
* **Sensory Staging**: Describe the heavy chill of the hall, the emerald mist swirling at knee height, and the eerie, untouched dignity of the fallen Dwarven captains. The slate is cold as mountain ice and leaves a faint smudge of sulfur on the finder's fingers.
* **Tactical Value to the Party**:
  1. **Solves the Vault Mystery**: Explicitly tells the players that *Durin's Axe* is inside Location 10 and that Location 9 (The King's Door) is currently sealed.
  2. **Points to Key Locations**: Directs the heroes to seek the *Mithril Key* among the Mordor Orc patrol (Grimnar / Grashnak's scout troop) and find the *Last Redoubt* in the Fifth Hall to retrieve the *King's Key*.
  3. **Unlocks the Craft Bypass Option**: Grants tactical advantages (+2d on Tumbler Alignment) if the players decide to pick the adamant lock via the Skill Endeavour.
