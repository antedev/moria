TACTICAL SQUAD DEPLOYMENT RULES
1. Gatehouse Garrison (2 Dwarves): Secures extraction redoubt. Adds +2 to final withdrawal and reduces Alert by -1 AP per Act.
2. Salvage Porter Squad (2 Dwarves): Carries up to 600 lbs of recovered wargear/mithril without penalizing hero Fatigue.
3. Shield-Wall Phalanx (Attached to Torvir): Absorbs 1 Piercing Blow per battle on behalf of a hero.
4. Crossbow Suppressing Fire: Strips 1 Hate point per round from enemy commanders on a successful War test.
BAND MARCHING & COMBAT RESOLUTION
• Marching Test: Khoril tests TRAVEL (HRT TN 16) or Band rolls MANOEUVRE (TN 15). Failure: +1 Alert Point.
• Band Clash in Combat: Roll Feat Die + WAR (3d) vs TN 15. Each success inflicts 4 Endurance damage on enemy minion mob; each 6 icon inflicts a Piercing Blow.
• Casualty Threshold: If $\ge 4$ companions become wounded, the Band becomes Weary (-1d to all rolls).

RUNEMASTER TRANSLATION (LORE / CRAFT TEST SUCCESS)

"The fire-demon’s breath fills the fourteenth hall... No helm nor cloth can stay the burning vapour. The Tunnel-guards stand fast, but their breath turns to ash where they stand.

Hear me, King Durin! The Marshal of the Armies has fallen in the Sixteenth Hall of the Fifth Deep, and his Iron Key was torn from his breast by the fleeing goblin-swarms. The Lower Vault cannot be opened without it!

Send the King’s Key from the Last Redoubt, or let the blood of Durin and the ancient Craft of Aulë command the adamant seal, else the Axe of the First Father is sealed in the dark forever..."

— Frár, Scribe of the Royal Arsenal (Year 1980 of the Third Age)
LOREMASTER INSTRUCTIONS & SKILL REVELATIONS
Skill Tested Required Check Lore & Clues Revealed to the Fellowship
LORE Wits TN 15/16 Identifies the script as Royal Khuzdul Cirth. Confirms the Marshal's Key was carried into the deep warrens (connecting toward Goblin Village or Udûn tracks), while the King's Key was sealed in the Last Redoubt (Old Moria).
CRAFT Strength TN 13/14 Recognizes the runic schematics sketched on the reverse of the slate: reveals the King's Door uses a dual-interlocking cam mechanism and can be bypassed via the Blood of Durin ritual (VALOUR test) if the King's Key is missing.
SCAN Wits TN 15 (Einar +2) Spots tiny mineral crystalline residue along the fracture line: indicates the poison gas pooled from lower ventilation shafts and can be filtered with tallow-greased linen soaked in spirits or athelas.

CRITICAL TACTICAL BYPASSES & CHOKEPOINTS
• The Flue Bypass: A narrow ventilation shaft connects Area 2 directly to Area 5, bypassing the central Mustering-Yard entirely (requires EXPLORE [WIT TN 15]).
• The Catwalk Overwatch: High stone walkways run above Area 6 and Area 1, accessible via stone rungs. Perfect for companion crossbow marksmen (Bláin & Bróga).
• The Keystone Collapse Point: Over Area 2, an unstable archway can be rigged to collapse during the final withdrawal, sealing the shafts against Malech's shock-troops.
