## 2. Standard Garrison & Patrol Adversaries

### Udûn Sniffers (Trackers of the Deep Slavers)
*Small, Sneering, Sharp-Scented*
* **Attribute Level**: 4 | **Endurance**: 16 | **Might**: 1 | **Hate**: 3
* **Parry**: +1 | **Armour**: 2d
* **Combat Proficiencies**: Serrated Spear 2 (Damage 4, Injury 14, Poisoned), Short Bow 2 (Damage 3, Injury 12)
* **Fell Abilities**:
  * *Blood-Scent*: Udûn sniffers ignore darkness penalties when tracking bleeding or weary foes.
  * *Orc-Poison*: Any hero who suffers an Injury from a spear must make a **HEALING / ENDURANCE** test (TN 14) or suffer 2 additional Endurance loss every 10 minutes.

### Orc Soldiers of Moria (Garrison Infantry)
*Medium, Fierce, Brutish*
* **Attribute Level**: 4 | **Endurance**: 18 | **Might**: 1 | **Hate**: 3
* **Parry**: +1 | **Armour**: 2d
* **Combat Proficiencies**: Bent Sword 2 (Damage 4, Injury 16), Spear 2 (Damage 4, Injury 14)
* **Fell Abilities**: *Denizen of the Dark* (Favoured attack rolls in complete darkness).

### Orc Guards (Malech's Heavy Shock-Troops)
*Medium, Armoured, Disciplined*
* **Attribute Level**: 5 | **Endurance**: 24 | **Might**: 1 | **Hate**: 4
* **Parry**: +2 | **Armour**: 3d (Heavy Dwarven Ring-mail)
* **Combat Proficiencies**: Heavy Cleaver 3 (Damage 5, Injury 18, Break Shield), Orc Bow 2 (Damage 4, Injury 14)
* **Fell Abilities**: *Shield-Wall* (When in pairs, they gain +2 Parry).

## 3. Subterranean Hazard Mechanics Matrix

| Hazard | Trigger Condition | Primary Skill Test | Failure Consequence | Mitigation / Counterplay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Balrog Toxic Gas** *(Breath of the Pit)* | Entering Poisoned Halls (Area 7/8) | **ENDURANCE TN 14** *(Hourly with masks)* | 2 Endurance loss, Weary condition, +1 Shadow | Wear masks soaked in healing herbs; Craft salve (Resistance 4, TN 14). |
| **Slag-Worm Collapse** | Loud noise or troll strike | **ATHLETICS TN 14** | 8 Damage (Injury 16), pinned under iron | Spot weak ceiling with **SCAN TN 12**; brace with iron spikes. |
| **Scrap-Traps** | Walking down main aisles in Area 3 | **SCAN TN 14** *(Einar +2)* | 4 Damage, makes loud crash (**+2 Alert Points**) | Step around via side aisles; disarm with **CRAFT TN 12**. |
| **Orc Desecration Idol** | Viewing the altar in Area 4 | **VALOUR TN 14** | 1 Shadow (Dread); Torvir tests to resist rage | Look away; recite Dwarven litanies of Durin (**LORE TN 12**). |
| **Iron-Scrap Floor** | Moving in Area 6 (Troll Lair) | **STEALTH TN 16** | Clatter awakens The Mauler (**+3 Alert Points**) | Move along perimeter catwalks; toss meat/distraction. |
