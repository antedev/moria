## 2. Masterwork Tunnel-Guard Relics

In addition to Durin's Axe, the Lower Armoury (Area 10) and Poisoned Halls (Area 7) contain the masterwork armaments of the Dwarven vanguard:

### 1. The Shield of the Deep Gate (Torvir or Einar)
* **Type**: Reinforced Great Shield | **Parry**: +4 (+2 base, +1 reward, +1 Moria-silver binding) | **Load**: 3
* **Enchanted Blessing (*Warded Against the Dark*)**: The bearer is immune to being knocked down or seized by Huge creatures (such as Cave-trolls or Slag-worms).

### 2. The Mattock of the Iron Vanguard (Torvir)
* **Type**: Two-handed Mattock | **Damage**: 7 | **Injury**: 20 | **Load**: 3
* **Enchanted Reward (*Breaker of Stone and Plate*)**: Attacks made with this mattock ignore 2 points of enemy Armour. Against structural obstacles (blast-doors, stone pillars), it deals triple damage.

### 3. The Mail of Unyielding Stone (Einar or Khoril)
* **Type**: Suit of Dwarf-forged Gromril Coat of Mail | **Protection**: 5d | **Load**: 10 (Extremely light for mail)
* **Enchanted Blessing (*Mountain-Forged*)**: The wearer gains +2d on Protection tests against environmental damage (fire, crushing stone, toxic fumes).

### 4. The Stolen Dagger of Durin (Recovered from Grimnar)
* **Type**: Dagger / Short Blade | **Damage**: 4 | **Injury**: 14 | **Load**: 0
* **Blessings**: *Keen*, *Luminous Starlight* (glows with a pale blue-white luminescence whenever Orcs or Trolls are within 50 paces, completely negating darkness penalties for the bearer).

## 3. The Greater Hoard of the Third Deep

The Lower Armoury contains **120 Treasure Points** of ancient wealth, organized into portable containers for the companion Dwarf Band:

* **3 Chests of Refined Mithril-Alloy Ingots** (40 Treasure Points each): Heavy, but worth a fortune in Erebor or the Blue Mountains.
* **40 Masterwork Dwarf-Forged Weapons** (Swords, Axes, Crossbows, and Mattocks): Enough to fully arm Balin's colony at the East-Gate Camp.
* **10 Hermetically Sealed Helmets of the Tunnel-Guards**: Equipped with fine brass mesh filters that grant immunity to smoke and toxic fumes.
