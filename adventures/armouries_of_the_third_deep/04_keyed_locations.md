## 1. Architectural Overview & Spatial Navigation

The **Armouries of the Third Deep** comprise an ancient subterranean military complex constructed during the First and Second Ages of Khazad-dûm. Situated four hundred feet below *The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard* and directly above the magma-warmed lower foundries, the complex was engineered to equip, train, and marshal the heavy vanguard of Durin's Folk during the wars against Morgoth and Sauron.

## 2. Global Tactical Rules Across All Keyed Locations

Every keyed location in this chapter interacts directly with the core systems established in Chapters 1, 2, and 3:

1. **The 4-Stage Alert Tracker**:
   - **Alert 0 (*Quiet Shadows*, 0–3 Noise)**: Stealth and Explore tests gain **+1d**. The Company automatically gains a **Surprise Round** against unaware sentries.
   - **Alert 1 (*Unease & Scent*, 4–7 Noise)**: Standard rolls. Roaming Udûn Sniffer patrols check the room on a Feat die result of the **Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$)**.
   - **Alert 2 (*Hunted & Barricaded*, 8–11 Noise)**: Awareness rolls to avoid ambushes lose **-1d**. Reinforced doors are barred. **Grimnar the Disgraced** actively stalks the party.
   - **Alert 3 (*Drums in the Deep*, 12+ Noise)**: Triggers an immediate **Revelation Episode**. A 6-round countdown begins before the Upper Gatehouse escape shafts are sealed.
2. **Sound Economy & Noise Generation**:
   - Routine whispering and shadow movement: **+0 Noise**.
   - Standard speech / opening stone chests: **+1 Noise**.
   - Melee combat round: **+1 Noise** (1-round quiet kill) to **+2 Noise** (clashing blades and shouted orders).
   - Extended combat (4+ rounds) or toppling stone idols: **+3 Noise** (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness).
   - Siege weapon firing / controlled cave-ins: **+4 Noise** (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness).
   - Sounding Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm*: **+5 Noise** (**Instant Alert Tier 3!** +2 Eye Awareness).
3. **Band Deployment Roles**:
   - **Forward Scout Screen** (Austri & Bróga): Advance 50 paces ahead; roll **Vigilance (2d6)** or **Manoeuvre (2d6)** vs TN 15 to spot traps and sentries.
   - **Shield-Wall Phalanx** (Dúrmer, Dolg, Bláin): Form front rank; roll **War (3d6)** vs TN 15 to bottle doorways and intercept melee attacks.
   - **Rearguard Choke Defense** (Bláin & Fáin): Held at Location 2 to guarantee the extraction corridor (+2 Band Readiness on escape).
   - **Heavy Salvage Porters** (Hjoldring & Dúrmer): Haul wargear from Location 8 (+50 Garrison Supply Points, shifts Band to Heavy Burden).
4. **Hero Signature Items & Flaws**:
   - **Einar's *The Broken Key***: Grants a permanent **+2 modifier / Advantage (roll 2 Feat dice, keep best)** on all **Scan** tests for traps, concealed doors, and mechanisms.
   - **Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm***: Grants **+1 to all Battle rolls** and enables the *Rally Companions* action, but its acoustic roar adds **+1 Alert** and **+2 Eye Awareness**.
   - **Torvir's *Curse of Vengeance***: Triggered by Orc desecrations and blasphemous idols; failing Dread tests sparks reckless rage.
   - **Einar's *Dragon-sickness***: Triggered by glittering gold-leaf, gems, and ancient hoards; failing tests compels him to covet and linger.

* **Role**: Infiltration Landing & Crossroads  
* **Elevation**: Upper Tier (Level 3A)  
* **Connections**:
  * *North*: Vertical Counterweight Shaft and ladder flues ascending 400 feet to *The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard* (Safe Haven).

  * *East*: Choked masonry gallery leading to a narrow ventilation crawlway that bypasses directly into Location 3 (*Explore TN 14*).
* **Baseline Threat**: Alert Tier 0 (Quiet Shadows). Two Udûn Sniffers stationed behind Pillar #4.

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *You step off the creaking hemp bucket-sling onto a high stone overlook, gazing across a cavernous subterranean yard carved from the black basalt of the mountain’s roots. Vast octagonal pillars, thick as ancient oaks and scored with jagged, vitrified scorch-marks from the first rising of Durin’s Bane, march into the suffocating gloom. Below lies a broad stone pavilion where the marshals of Khazad-dûm once assembled ten thousand axe-bearers. Now, broken spears and rusted iron shards lie scattered across cracked flagstones like dead leaves, and a chill, sulfurous draft whispers through the darkness.*

#### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Total subterranean darkness. Faint, ghostly green bio-luminescent lichen clings in patches to the highest ceiling vaults forty feet overhead. Lantern beams cast long, dancing shadows behind the colossal octagonal pillars.
* **Drafts**: A steady, cold updraft pulls from the lower vents and shafts, carrying the bitter, acrid tang of brimstone, cold slag, and ancient soot.
* **Echoes**: Extreme acoustic resonance. The high basalt vault amplifies metal-on-stone contact; an unpadded mail-shirt or dropped coin rings out like a bell for a hundred paces.
* **Smells**: Old coal soot, damp stone, stale tallow grease, and the faint, biting ozone tang of rock vitrified by demonic fire.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **The Scorched Pavilion**: A raised platform of dark granite (4 feet high) with shallow steps on four sides. The center bears the glassy, vitrified footprint of Durin's Bane—a four-foot circle of fused black glass where the demon stood during the Fall. The stone parapet grants **Half Cover (+1 Parry)** during ranged exchanges.
2. **Elevated Sentry Galleries**: Narrow stone ledges running along the east and west walls ten feet above the yard floor, accessed by shallow recessed wall-rungs. Provides an ideal vantage point for archers (Rearward stance without distance penalty).
3. **The Udûn Sentry Post**: Tucked behind Pillar #4 sits a crude shelter of stitched cave-bear hides and blackened cedar poles. Inside, two **Udûn Sniffers** crouch over a smoldering brazier of dried bat dung, squabbling over knucklebone dice.
4. **Grik's Shadow Alcove**: Concealed within a narrow drainage slit beneath the northern overlook perches **Grik the Skulker**, clutching his knees and watching the sentries with malevolent yellow eyes.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Scan (TN 14)**: Spot the Udûn sentry post behind Pillar #4 and identify the patrol timing before stepping onto the open floor.  
  * *Einar's The Broken Key*: Grants **+2 modifier / Advantage (roll 2 Feat dice, take best)**. On a Gandalf rune ($\mathbf{G}$), Einar also spots Grik's glowing eyes in the northern drainage slit!
* **Stealth (TN 14) / Band Manoeuvre (2d6 vs TN 15)**: Guide the expedition across the open flagstones to the southern archway without alerting the sentries.  
  * *Failure*: A dwarf's shield scrapes an iron stanchion, generating **+1 Noise Point** (advances Alert Tracker toward Tier 1).
* **Battle / Old Lore (TN 14)**: Inspect the vitrified scorch marks on the pavilion. Reveals the ancient tactical kill-zones designed into the hall and confirms that Durin's Bane swept through this hall over a thousand years ago heading south.
* **Persuade / Riddle (TN 14)**: Parley with Grik the Skulker if he is discovered.  
  * *Success*: Grik offers valuable intelligence: he confirms that **Grimnar** is prowling the lower levels and reveals that the **Marshal's Key** was stolen centuries ago and carried down toward Goblin Village. Grik will trade a crude parchment map of Location 3's tripwires for 5 silver pennies or two days of Dwarf tobacco.

#### Tactical Band Deployment & Companion Operations
* **Forward Scout Screen (Austri & Bróga)**: Advance along the high eastern gallery. Roll **Band Vigilance (2d6 vs TN 15)**. Success spots the Udûn sentries instantly and allows the Company to bypass or neutralize them in a silent surprise round.
* **Marching Discipline (Khoril's Leadership)**: Khoril rolls **Battle (TN 14)** before moving. Success allows the entire squad of 10 Dwarves to traverse the 100-pace yard in complete silence (**0 Noise generated**).

#### Sound & Alert Tracker Impact
* Crossing the yard in silent shadow-crawl: **+0 Noise**.
* Engaging the 2 Udûn Sniffers and slaying them within 1 round: **+1 Noise**.
* Letting an Udûn Sniffer blow a bone whistle or escape south: **+3 Noise (+1 Alert Tier, +2 Eye Awareness)**.
* Blowing Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm*: **+5 Noise (Instant Alert 3 / Drums in the Deep!)**.

#### Loot & Discoveries
* **Udûn Sentry Pouch**: 8 crude iron pence, a bag of greasy dried mushrooms, and a jagged bone-handled skewer.
* **Scorched Rubble Cache** (*Scan TN 14*): A rusted iron coffer containing 3 heavy Dwarven throwing mattocks (Damage 5, Injury 16) and an intact flask of *Dwarven Lantern Oil* (burns for 6 hours).

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *Before you stands the inner gatehouse of the Upper Armouries, a monumental bulwark of dressed granite built into the living rock. Its massive blast-doors—wrought from adamant-plated star-iron four inches thick—lie buckled outward into the corridor, their hinges sheared by ancient explosive fury and battering engines. Heavy iron chains and massive counterweight blocks hang limply from ceiling winches above. Narrow murder-holes and arrow embrasures pierce the high battlements, commanding the ramp below. Though battered by siege, the masonry remains unbroken—a natural Gibraltar in the throat of the deep.*

#### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Deep gloom. The narrow ceiling embrasures admit no light from above, creating deep shadow banks within the gatehouse archway.
* **Drafts**: A violent, whistling draft funnels through the buckled adamant doors, moaning with a low, rhythmic hum like an ancient horn.
* **Echoes**: The thick granite walls heavily muffle sound from within, deadening spoken conversation to a flat, dry whisper that does not carry into adjacent halls.
* **Smells**: Pulverized stone dust, dry iron rust, dried bat guano, and the crisp, clean draft of deep mountain rock.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **Buckled Adamant Blast-Doors**: The two massive door leaves are warped outward at a 45-degree angle. By spending 15 minutes and using iron wedges and timber beams, the Company can wedge them into a rigid barricade, creating an impassable barrier against frontal charges.
2. **The Keystone Winch Trap**: Directly above the southern archway hangs a cracked granite keystone (weighing three tons) held in place by an ancient iron winch cable. The cable runs into the right-hand guard turret. A trip-rope or lever pull releases the winch, dropping the keystone and causing a **Controlled Cave-In** across the passage.
   * *Cave-In Damage*: Inflicts **30 Damage** (Protection roll 4d vs Injury TN 18) to all targets beneath it and permanently seals the southern rampway with ten tons of rubble.
3. **Murder-Hole Defense Turrets**: Two elevated flanking guard chambers accessed by narrow stone spiral stairs. Companions stationed here have **Total Cover (+3 Parry, immune to ranged attacks from below)** and can fire down through murder-holes into the southern approach.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Craft (TN 14) / Band Expertise (2d6 vs TN 15)**: Fortify the gatehouse using scrap cedar balks, iron pitons, and stone rubble.  
  * *Success*: Establishes the gatehouse as a **Fortified Forward Redoubt**. While held by 2–3 companions, the Company's retreat route to Thrym's Haven is 100% secure, and the Band gains **+2 Band Readiness** during Act III's *Fighting Withdrawal*.
* **Craft (TN 16) / Battle (TN 14)**: Inspect and rig the Keystone Winch Trap with a quick-release tripwire.  
  * *Success*: The trap can be triggered instantly with a single reaction during the fighting withdrawal, crushing the lead elements of pursuing Orc warbands.
* **Explore (TN 14)**: Discover a narrow maintenance flue behind the left turret that crawls around the collapsed section of the eastern wall, bypassing Location 3 entirely (*Einar +2 from The Broken Key*).

#### Tactical Band Deployment & Companion Operations
* **Rearguard Choke Point Defense (Critical Decision)**:
  * The party may elect to leave **2 to 3 veteran companions** (recommended: **Bláin the Cleaver** with his heavy axe and **Fáin Iron-Visor** with his Dwarven bow) to garrison the Upper Gatehouse.
  * *Mechanical Benefits*:
    1. Guarantees that the vertical escape shaft back to Thrym's Haven cannot be captured or cut off by wandering Udûn patrols.
    2. Grants an automatic **+2 bonus to Band Readiness** during the final extraction.
    3. Allows the rearguard to man the murder-holes and keystone trap to cover the heroes' retreat.
* **Phalanx Fortification (Dúrmer & Hjoldring)**: Dúrmer (*Mighty*) and Hjoldring (*Smith*) haul massive iron plates from the broken doors to build an arrow-proof firing slit across the main threshold.

#### Sound & Alert Tracker Impact
* Shoring timber and hammering iron wedges quietly: **+1 Noise**.
* Sledgehammering stone or forcing rusty winches without grease: **+3 Noise (+1 Alert Tier)**.
* Triggering the Keystone Cave-In: **+4 Noise (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness)**.

#### Loot & Discoveries
* **Gatehouse Armoire**: A sealed bronze wall-locker (*Burglary TN 14* to open; *Bróga gains +1d*) containing 2 bundles of masterwork Dwarven arrows (24 arrows, +1 to ranged Damage), 4 iron grappling hooks with 50 feet of braided hemp rope, and a stone flask of *Dwarven Blasting Pitch* (alchemical accelerant).

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *You descend into a long, low-ceilinged vault where double rows of stone weapon racks stand stripped and desolate, their bronze clasps and velvet linings torn away centuries ago. The floor is choked with the detritus of Orc scavenging: shattered wooden shields, gnawed goat bones, and piles of rusted iron caltrops. Across the central thoroughfare, thin black cords of twisted sinew stretch taut between iron stanchions, rigged to counterweighted scythe-blades dripping with a foul, glistening black pitch.*

#### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Complete subterranean blackness. The iridescent, oily sheen of black Orc-venom on the tripwire blades catches lantern rays with a greasy purple glare.
* **Drafts**: Stagnant, dead air smelling of damp rot and stagnant grease; no perceptible breeze stirs the dust.
* **Echoes**: The low stone ceiling (12 feet high) creates a sharp, slapping echo. Every kicked pebble or scuffed iron boot rattles loudly across the floor.
* **Smells**: The sickening, sweet stench of rotting marrow, stale Orc dung, and the pungent, bitter almond tang of Orcish black venom.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **The Scythe-Blade Scrap-Traps**: Two interlocking tripwire lines stretched 4 inches above the floor across the main aisle. Stepping on a wire releases heavy overhead scythe-arms made of scavenged Dwarven broad-axes lashed to iron bars.
   * *Trap Stats*: Attack roll 4d vs **Target Strength TN + Shield**. On a hit, inflicts **14 Damage** and a **Piercing Blow (Injury TN 16)**. The blade is coated in **Black Orc-Venom** (*see below*).
2. **Black Orc-Venom Vats**: Two rusted iron cauldrons in the corner filled with coagulated viper-bile, nightshade resin, and coal tar.
   * *Poison Effect*: A character wounded by a poisoned blade must pass a **Healing (TN 14)** or **Endurance (TN 14)** test. Failure inflicts the **Poisoned condition** (the character becomes *Weary* and loses 2 Endurance per hour until treated with First Aid).
3. **Concealed Stone Arming Cache**: Beneath stripped weapon rack #12, a loose floor-flag bears a faint hammer-rune at its corner.
   * *Contents*: Conceals a sealed cedar chest containing 4 pristine Dwarven war-mattocks and 6 steel throwing knives untouched by the Orcs.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Scan (TN 14)**: Detect the taut sinew tripwires and caltrop fields before stepping into the crossfire.  
  * *Einar's The Broken Key*: Grants **+2 modifier / Advantage (roll 2 Feat dice, take best)**. Einar immediately identifies the counterweight pivot points.
* **Burglary (TN 14) / Craft (TN 14) / Band Expertise (2d6 vs TN 15)**: Disarm the tripwires and counterweight pivots silently without springing the blades.  
  * *Failure*: The trap triggers! The scythe blade swings down, attacking the lead character and generating **+2 Noise Points**.
* **Healing (TN 14) / Craft (TN 14)**: Safely scrape and harvest the black venom from the vats into glass vials.  
  * *Success*: Yields **3 doses of Black Orc-Venom**. Applying venom to a blade increases its **Injury Rating by +2** for one combat encounter.
* **Craft (TN 14)**: Re-arm the scythe traps to face backwards toward Location 2, creating an active defensive snare against pursuing Orcs during the withdrawal.

#### Tactical Band Deployment & Companion Operations
* **Forward Scout Screen (Austri & Bróga)**: Bróga (*Vaultbreaker*) uses his copper-ringed picks to wedge the trap pivots while Austri maps the safe path around the caltrops.
* **Salvage Assessment (Hjoldring)**: Hjoldring (*Smith*) examines the stripped racks, confirming that the Orcs only carried away crude iron weapons, leaving the heavy gromril storehouses further down undisturbed.

#### Sound & Alert Tracker Impact
* Silently creeping through and disarming traps: **+0 Noise**.
* Accidental trap trigger (scythe blade smashing stone): **+2 Noise (+1/2 Alert Tier)**.
* Searching through scrap heaps loudly: **+1 Noise**.

#### Loot & Discoveries
* **Concealed Arming Cache**: 4 Dwarven War-Mattocks (Damage 6, Injury 18, Break Shield) and 6 Throwing Knives (Damage 3, Injury 14).
* **Scavenged Orc Pouch**: 14 brass coins minted in Gundabad, a piece of dried troll-flesh, and an iron key that fits the lockbox in Location 8.

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
