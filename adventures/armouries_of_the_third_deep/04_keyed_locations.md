## 1. Architectural Overview & Spatial Navigation

The **Armouries of the Third Deep** comprise an ancient subterranean military complex constructed during the First and Second Ages of Khazad-dûm. Situated four hundred feet below *The Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard* and directly above the magma-warmed lower foundries, the complex was engineered to equip, train, and marshal the heavy vanguard of Durin's Folk during the wars against Morgoth and Sauron.

## 2. Global Tactical Rules Across All Keyed Locations

Every keyed location in this chapter interacts directly with the core systems established in Chapters 1, 2, and 3:

1. **The 4-Stage Alert Tracker**:
   - **Alert 0 (*Quiet Shadows*, 0–3 Noise)**: Stealth and Explore tests gain **+1d**. The Company automatically gains a **Surprise Round** against unaware sentries.
   - **Alert 1 (*Unease & Scent*, 4–7 Noise)**: Standard rolls. Roaming Udûn Sniffer patrols check the room on a Feat die result of the **Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$)**.
   - **Alert 2 (*Hunted & Barricaded*, 8–11 Noise)**: Awareness rolls to avoid ambushes lose **-1d**. Reinforced doors are barred. **Grimnar the Disgraced** actively stalks the party.
   - **Alert 3 (*Drums in the Deep*, 12+ Noise)**: Triggers an immediate **Revelation Episode**. A 6-round countdown begins before the Upper Gatehouse escape shafts are sealed.
2. **Sound Economy & Noise Generation**:
   - Routine whispering and shadow movement: **+0 Noise**.
   - Standard speech / opening stone chests: **+1 Noise**.
   - Melee combat round: **+1 Noise** (1-round quiet kill) to **+2 Noise** (clashing blades and shouted orders).
   - Extended combat (4+ rounds) or toppling stone idols: **+3 Noise** (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness).
   - Siege weapon firing / controlled cave-ins: **+4 Noise** (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness).
   - Sounding Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm*: **+5 Noise** (**Instant Alert Tier 3!** +2 Eye Awareness).
3. **Band Deployment Roles**:
   - **Forward Scout Screen** (Austri & Bróga): Advance 50 paces ahead; roll Band **VIGILANCE** (2d) or Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d) against **Band TN 15** to spot traps and sentries.
   - **Shield-Wall Phalanx** (Dúrmer, Dolg, Bláin): Form front rank; roll Band **WAR** (3d) against **Band TN 15** to bottle doorways and intercept melee attacks.
   - **Rearguard Choke Defense** (Bláin & Fáin): Held at Location 2 to guarantee the extraction corridor (+2 Band Readiness on escape).
   - **Heavy Salvage Porters** (Hjoldring & Dúrmer): Haul wargear from Location 8 (40 Dwarf Mail-shirts, 30 Shields, 50 Mattocks/Axes; shifts Band to Heavy Burden).
4. **Hero Signature Items & Flaws**:
   - **Einar's *The Broken Key***: Grants **Favoured** rolls (roll 2 Feat dice, take the better result) on all **SCAN** tests for traps, concealed doors, and mechanisms.
   - **Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm***: Grants **+1d to all BATTLE and RALLY rolls** and enables the *Rally Companions* action, but its acoustic roar adds **+1 Alert Tier** and **+2 Eye Awareness**.
   - **Torvir's *Curse of Vengeance***: Triggered by Orc desecrations and blasphemous idols; failing Dread tests sparks reckless rage.
   - **Einar's *Dragon-sickness***: Triggered by glittering gold-leaf, gems, and ancient hoards; failing tests compels him to covet and linger.

* **Elevation**: Upper Tier (Level 3A)

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *You step off the creaking hemp bucket-sling onto a high stone overlook, gazing across a cavernous subterranean yard carved from the black basalt of the mountain’s roots. Vast octagonal pillars, thick as ancient oaks and scored with jagged, vitrified scorch-marks from the first rising of Durin’s Bane, march into the suffocating gloom. Below lies a broad stone pavilion where the marshals of Khazad-dûm once assembled ten thousand axe-bearers. Now, broken spears and rusted iron shards lie scattered across cracked flagstones like dead leaves, and a chill, sulfurous draft whispers through the darkness.*

#### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Total subterranean darkness. Faint, ghostly green bio-luminescent lichen clings in patches to the highest ceiling vaults forty feet overhead. Lantern beams cast long, dancing shadows behind the colossal octagonal pillars.
* **Drafts**: A steady, cold updraft pulls from the lower vents and shafts, carrying the bitter, acrid tang of brimstone, cold slag, and ancient soot.
* **Echoes**: Extreme acoustic resonance. The high basalt vault amplifies metal-on-stone contact; an unpadded mail-shirt or dropped coin rings out like a bell for a hundred paces.
* **Smells**: Old coal soot, damp stone, stale tallow grease, and the faint, biting ozone tang of rock vitrified by demonic fire.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **The Scorched Pavilion**: A raised platform of dark granite (4 feet high) with shallow steps on four sides. The center bears the glassy, vitrified footprint of Durin's Bane—a four-foot circle of fused black glass where the demon stood during the Fall. The stone parapet grants **Half Cover (+1 Parry)** during ranged exchanges.
2. **Elevated Sentry Galleries**: Narrow stone ledges running along the east and west walls ten feet above the yard floor, accessed by shallow recessed wall-rungs. Provides an ideal vantage point for archers (Rearward stance without distance penalty).
3. **The Udûn Sentry Post**: Tucked behind Pillar #4 sits a crude shelter of stitched cave-bear hides and blackened cedar poles. Inside, two **Udûn Sniffers** crouch over a smoldering brazier of dried bat dung, squabbling over knucklebone dice.
4. **Grik's Shadow Alcove**: Concealed within a narrow drainage slit beneath the northern overlook perches **Grik the Skulker**, clutching his knees and watching the sentries with malevolent yellow eyes.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Perimeter Infiltration — STEALTH (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**: Slip around the perimeter pillars to the First Armoury (Location 3) or Broken Hall (Location 4) without alerting sentries.
  * *Modifiers*: Alert Tier 0 grants **+1d**; massive basalt pillars provide total cover.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: A companion's boot or shield clips an iron brazier stanchion, generating **+1 Noise Point** (advancing the Alert Tracker) and alerting the sniffing hounds.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The Company moves like smoke across the basalt floor; generates **+0 Noise** and grants **+1d** to the lead hero's next test in Location 2 or 3.
    * **66**: Flawless silent infiltration; bypasses sentries completely and identifies the optimal rear ambush angle.
* **Overlook Sentry Scouting — SCAN (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**: Spot the Udûn sentry post behind Pillar #4 and identify patrol timing before stepping onto the open floor.
  * *Modifiers*: Einar invoking *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured** (roll two Feat dice, keep higher).
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Sentry post is not spotted before entering open ground; Company loses the surprise round if combat starts.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Discovers the 2 Udûn Sniffers dozing behind Pillar #4, notes their bone-dice distraction, and spots Grik the Skulker hiding in the northern drainage slit.
    * **66**: Uncovers a discarded Orc message scroll regarding friction between Malech and Thu the Firespeaker (*"Malech demands more iron from the deep foundries. Let the Fire-priests choke on their smoke"*).
* **Ambush Assault — BATTLE (Heart TN: Torvir 18, Einar 17, Khoril 16)**: Attack sentries from surprise from the high balconies or unseen pillars.
  * *Modifiers*: Attacking from surprise grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Sentries raise an alarm shout before being engaged (**+2 Noise Points**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Eliminates 2 sentries immediately before they sound a horn (**+1 Noise Point** total for a 1-round quiet kill).
    * **66**: The entire sentry squad is eliminated in absolute silence (**+0 Noise**).
* **Ancient Scorch Analysis — LORE (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16) or BATTLE (Heart TN: Torvir 18, Einar 17, Khoril 16)**: Inspect the vitrified scorch marks on the pavilion.
  * *Modifiers*: Torvir invoking *Enemy-lore (Orcs)* or *Dwarven Lore* gains **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Inconclusive inspection; wastes 10 minutes.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Confirms scorch marks are vitrified Balrog footprints from 1980 T.A. and identifies ancient engineered Dwarven defensive kill-zones across the plaza.
* **Parley with Grik the Skulker — PERSUADE or RIDDLE (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**: Parley with Grik if discovered.
  * *Modifiers*: Offering Dwarf tobacco or 5 silver pennies grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Grik panics and darts into his 6-inch drainage chute, hissing a warning to the sentries (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Grik shares vital intelligence: confirms **Grimnar** is prowling Level 3C, reveals the **Marshal's Key** was carried toward Goblin Village, and trades a crude charcoal sketch of Location 3's tripwires for 5 silver pennies or tobacco.

#### Tactical Band Deployment & Companion Operations
* **Forward Scout Screen (Austri & Bróga)**: Advance along the high eastern gallery. Roll Band **VIGILANCE** (2d) against **Band TN 15**. Success spots the Udûn sentries instantly and allows the Company to bypass or neutralize them in a silent surprise round.
* **Marching Discipline (Khoril's Leadership)**: Khoril rolls **TRAVEL or ENHEARTEN (Heart TN: 16)** invoking *Leadership* Trait (**+1d**), or Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d) against **Band TN 15**. Success allows the entire squad of 10 Dwarves to traverse the 100-pace yard in complete silence (**+0 Noise generated**).

#### Sound & Alert Tracker Impact
* Crossing the yard in silent shadow-crawl: **+0 Noise**.
* Engaging the 2 Udûn Sniffers and slaying them within 1 round: **+1 Noise**.
* Letting an Udûn Sniffer blow a bone whistle or escape south: **+3 Noise (+1 Alert Tier, +2 Eye Awareness)**.
* Blowing Khoril's *Battle-horn of the Realm*: **+5 Noise (Instant Alert 3 / Drums in the Deep!)**.

#### Loot & Discoveries
* **Udûn Sentry Pouch**: 8 crude iron pence, a bag of greasy dried mushrooms, and a jagged bone-handled skewer.
* **Scorched Rubble Cache** (**SCAN** [Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16]): A rusted iron coffer containing 3 heavy Dwarven throwing mattocks (Damage 5, Injury 16) and an intact flask of *Dwarven Lantern Oil* (burns for 6 hours).

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *Before you stands the inner gatehouse of the Upper Armouries, a monumental bulwark of dressed granite built into the living rock. Its massive blast-doors—wrought from adamant-plated star-iron four inches thick—lie buckled outward into the corridor, their hinges sheared by ancient explosive fury and battering engines. Heavy iron chains and massive counterweight blocks hang limply from ceiling winches above. Narrow murder-holes and arrow embrasures pierce the high battlements, commanding the ramp below. Though battered by siege, the masonry remains unbroken—a natural Gibraltar in the throat of the deep.*

#### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Deep gloom. The narrow ceiling embrasures admit no light from above, creating deep shadow banks within the gatehouse archway.
* **Drafts**: A violent, whistling draft funnels through the buckled adamant doors, moaning with a low, rhythmic hum like an ancient horn.
* **Echoes**: The thick granite walls heavily muffle sound from within, deadening spoken conversation to a flat, dry whisper that does not carry into adjacent halls.
* **Smells**: Pulverized stone dust, dry iron rust, dried bat guano, and the crisp, clean draft of deep mountain rock.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **Buckled Adamant Blast-Doors**: The two massive door leaves are warped outward at a 45-degree angle. By spending 10 minutes and using iron wedges and timber beams, the Company can wedge them into a rigid barricade, creating an impassable barrier against frontal charges.
2. **The Keystone Winch Trap**: Directly above the southern archway hangs a cracked granite keystone (weighing three tons) held in place by an ancient iron winch cable. The cable runs into the right-hand guard turret. A trip-rope or lever pull releases the winch, dropping the keystone and causing a **Controlled Cave-In** across the passage.
   * *Cave-In Damage*: Inflicts **30 Damage** (Protection roll 4d vs Injury TN 18) to all targets beneath it and permanently seals the southern rampway with ten tons of rubble (**+4 Noise Points**).
3. **Murder-Hole Defense Turrets**: Two elevated flanking guard chambers accessed by narrow stone spiral stairs. Companions stationed here have **Total Cover (+3 Parry, immune to ranged attacks from below)** and can fire down through murder-holes into the southern approach.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Skill Endeavour: Fortifying the Forward Redoubt (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Bracing the buckled adamant blast-doors with cedar balks, iron pitons, and granite blocks.
  * *Resistance*: **3**. Each test represents 10 minutes of disciplined labor.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT** (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13), **ATHLETICS** (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13), **BATTLE** (Heart TN: Torvir 18, Einar 17, Khoril 16).
  * *Modifiers*: Hjoldring (*Smith*) or Fáin assisting grants **+1d**; Dúrmer (*Mighty*) grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Sledgehammer slips or timber creaks loudly (**+1 Noise Point** per failed attempt); if unfinished after 3 attempts, the barricade is partial (+1 Parry instead of Total Cover).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Contributes 3 points of Resistance (completes the endeavour in a single 10-minute turn!).
  * *Outcome on Success*: Establishes the Gatehouse as a **Fortified Forward Redoubt**. Rearguard garrison (Bláin & Fáin) receives **Total Cover (+3 Parry, immune to lower missile fire)** and adds **+2 Band Readiness** to the final Fighting Withdrawal.
* **Rigging the Keystone Winch Trap — CRAFT (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13) or BATTLE (Heart TN: Torvir 18, Einar 17, Khoril 16)**:
  * *Modifiers*: **Ill-favoured** if attempted in pitch darkness without lantern light.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The rusted winch jams or slips, dropping iron flakes (**+1 Noise Point**) and requiring a reset.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Rigs a hair-trigger trip-rope. During withdrawal, triggering the trap is a free combat reaction, dropping 3 tons of granite (**30 Damage, Protection 4d vs Injury TN 18**) and permanently sealing the southern rampway with 10 tons of rubble (**+4 Noise Points**).
* **Discovering the Maintenance Flue — EXPLORE (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**:
  * *Modifiers*: Einar invoking *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Flue entrance behind left turret remains hidden; Company must proceed down the main ramp into Location 3.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Locates the narrow crawlway bypassing Location 3 directly into Location 5.
* **Opening the Armoire Wall-Locker — CRAFT (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13) or STEALTH (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**:
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait (or Bróga's *Vaultbreaker* gift) grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Heavy bronze lock mechanism jams solid; forcing it requires iron crowbars (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Silent opening; recovers 24 masterwork arrows (+1 Ranged Damage), 4 iron grappling hooks with 50ft rope, and a flask of *Dwarven Blasting Pitch*.

#### Tactical Band Deployment & Companion Operations
* **Rearguard Choke Point Defense (Critical Decision)**:
  * The party may elect to leave **2 to 3 veteran companions** (recommended: **Bláin the Cleaver** with his heavy axe and **Fáin Iron-Visor** with his Dwarven bow) to garrison the Upper Gatehouse.
  * *Mechanical Benefits*:
    1. Guarantees that the vertical escape shaft back to Thrym's Haven cannot be captured or cut off by wandering Udûn patrols.
    2. Grants an automatic **+2 bonus to Band Readiness** during the final extraction.
    3. Allows the rearguard to man the murder-holes and keystone trap to cover the heroes' retreat.
* **Phalanx Fortification (Dúrmer & Hjoldring)**: Dúrmer (*Mighty*) and Hjoldring (*Smith*) haul massive iron plates from the broken doors to build an arrow-proof firing slit across the main threshold.

#### Sound & Alert Tracker Impact
* Shoring timber and hammering iron wedges quietly: **+1 Noise**.
* Sledgehammering stone or forcing rusty winches without grease: **+3 Noise (+1 Alert Tier)**.
* Triggering the Keystone Cave-In: **+4 Noise (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness)**.

#### Loot & Discoveries
* **Gatehouse Armoire**: A sealed bronze wall-locker containing 2 bundles of masterwork Dwarven arrows (24 arrows, +1 to ranged Damage), 4 iron grappling hooks with 50 feet of braided hemp rope, and a stone flask of *Dwarven Blasting Pitch* (alchemical accelerant).

#### Boxed Read-Aloud Text
> *The King’s Door glides inward on silent, counterweighted pivots, revealing the inner sanctum of Khazad-dûm’s royal treasury. In the center of the hall, resting upon a raised dais of mirror-polished black marble and bathed in a shaft of pale starlight, lies Durin’s Axe. #### GM Sensory Reference Bullets
* **Lighting**: Radiant gold and silver luminescence radiating from *Durin’s Axe* and the polished mithril filigree. The chamber seems to glow with an inner, sacred light.
* **Drafts**: Completely still, crisp, and sweet. The air smells like fresh mountain snow, cedar incense, and cold spring water.
* **Echoes**: Deep, solemn acoustics. Footsteps land with a quiet, ringing reverence like footsteps in a sacred cathedral.
* **Smells**: Pure cedar oil, gold dust, ancient resin, and clean mountain wind.

#### Interactive Environmental Features
1. **The Dais of Durin & Durin's Axe**:
   * A hexagonal plinth of black marble inscribed with the names of the First Six Kings of Durin's Line. Resting upon it is **Durin’s Axe** (see full profile in Chapter 6).
   * *Claiming the Relic*: Lifting the axe from its plinth releases an immense harmonic chime of stone that echoes throughout the mountain.
   * *Strategic Eye Awareness Trigger*: Claiming Durin's Axe instantly adds **+4 to Strategic Eye Awareness** and alerts every Orc chieftain in the Third Deep!
2. **The Three Royal Relic Coffers**:
   * *Coffer 1 — Shield of the Deep Gate*: A massive Great Shield of star-iron and mithril (+3 Parry, Load 3, *Unyielding* trait: wearer cannot be knocked down or Seized).
   * *Coffer 2 — Mattock of Moria-Silver*: A two-handed heavy war-mattock forged of mithril-steel (Damage 8, Injury 18, *Gleaming Edge*: attacks in darkness are Favoured).
   * *Coffer 3 — Mail of Unyielding Stone*: A suit of interlocking gromril-mail (Protection 5d, Load 12, *Impenetrable*: spend 1 Hope to reduce Injury severity by one step).
3. **The Vault Blast Barricade**: The inner adamant portcullis can be locked from within using a heavy star-iron wheel-bolt.
   * *Safe Redoubt*: Allows the Company to take a secure **30-minute Short Rest**, expend Fellowship Points, restore Hope, and organize their marching order before commencing the *Fighting Withdrawal*.

#### TOR 2e Skill Tests & Mechanics
* **Contemplating the Royal Relic — AWE or SONG (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13)**: Gaze upon the sacred relic without being overwhelmed by weeping reverence.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Overwhelmed by sorrow and reverent weeping; suffers the Daunted condition for 1 hour.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Heart filled with ancestral majesty; clears all *Weary* conditions from all Player-Heroes and restores **+2 Hope Points**.
* **Resisting Dragon-Sickness & Greed — Shadow Test (Heart TN: Einar 17 or Wits TN: Einar 15)**: Resist the overwhelming allure of the Greater Hoard.
  * *Modifiers*: Einar's *Dragon-sickness* Flaw makes this test **Ill-favoured** (-1d).
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Einar gains **2 Shadow Points (Greed)** and becomes compelled to stuff every golden goblet and mithril ingot into his pack, shifting Band status to Heavy Burden.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Einar's wisdom prevails over avarice; he focuses solely on securing the royal relics and mithril ingots.
* **Disengaging the Runic Stasis Field — CRAFT (Strength TN: Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13) or LORE (Wits TN: Torvir 15, Einar 15, Khoril 16)**: Safely disengage the runic stasis field surrounding Durin's Axe before lifting it.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The stasis field discharges with a thunderous electrical snap, inflicting 5 Damage to the lifter and generating **+2 Noise Points**.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The stasis field dissipates in complete silence; Durin's Axe is lifted cleanly from the black marble anvil.
