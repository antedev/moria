## 3. Session-by-Session GM Pacing Playbook

```
========================================================================================
                          SESSION 1: INFILTRATION & THE MUSTERING YARD
========================================================================================
[OBJECTIVE]: Descend from Thrym's Haven, secure the Upper Gatehouse, and infiltrate Area 1.

1. THE OPENING (15 min):
   • Describe the descent down the dark, vertical iron rung ladders from Thistlebeard's tower.
   • Khoril makes the opening March Test (TRAVEL [Heart TN 16] or Band MANOEUVRE [2d vs Band TN 15])
     to maintain squad silence.
   • Arrive at Area 2 (Upper Gatehouse). The party decides whether to garrison 2 Dwarves here.

2. THE MUSTERING-YARD ENCOUNTER (45 min):
   • Reveal the massive basalt crossroads (Area 1). Describe the smouldering Orc braziers.
   • Torvir and Einar scout ahead. Einar rolls SCAN (Wits TN 15, Favoured via The Broken Key)
     to spot the Udûn sentries.
   • Players choose: Silent perimeter sneak (STEALTH [Wits TN]) or sudden ambush (BATTLE [Heart TN]).
   • If combat occurs, resolve quickly. Emphasize keeping sentries from blowing alarm horns!

3. BRANCHING CHOICES (60 min):
   • Party investigates Area 3 (First Armoury: scrap-traps) or Area 5 (Second Armoury: siege engines).
   • Fáin and Hjoldring can repair a ballista, planting the seeds for Act III tactics.
   • End Session 1 peering into the heavy shadows of the Broken Hall or the glowing green mist.
========================================================================================
```

```
========================================================================================
                       SESSION 2: THE DESPOILED HALLS & THE POISON HEIST
========================================================================================
[OBJECTIVE]: Traverse the Broken Hall, brave the Balrog's toxic mist, and solve the Key quest.

1. THE BROKEN HALL (30 min):
   • Describe the desecrated war museum and the grotesque 12-foot bone idol of Morgoth.
   • Call for Dread tests (VALOUR vs Heart TN: Torvir 18, Einar 17, Khoril 16).
     Roleplay Torvir's burning desire for vengeance vs. silence.
   • Einar deciphers the ancient relief pointing to the Last Redoubt.

2. THE POISONED HALLS (60 min):
   • Describe the heavy ochre mist pooling waist-deep (Area 7).
   • Heroes don protective masks soaked in herbs/liquor. Protection test vs Strength TN
     (Torvir 13, Einar 14, Khoril 13).
   • Moving among the preserved Dwarven knights. Describe the eerie solemnity of the scene.
   • Find the Scribe's Dying Letter: reveals the Marshal's Key was lost down below.

3. THE MARSHAL'S KEY HEIST (45 min):
   • Grik the Goblin appears on a high ledge, or an Udûn sniffer patrol approaches from the shaft.
   • Social / Tactical Scene: Negotiating with Grik (PERSUADE/RIDDLE [Wits TN]) or executing
     a silent takedown of the Udûn sniffers.
   • Secure the Marshal's Key!
   • End Session 2 standing before the colossal adamant slab of The King's Door.
========================================================================================
```

```
========================================================================================
                      SESSION 3: THE TROLL, THE VAULT & THE EXTRACTION
========================================================================================
[OBJECTIVE]: Defeat/bypass The Mauler, unlock the Vault, claim Durin's Axe, and escape!

1. THE CLASH WITH THE MAULER (45 min):
   • The party enters Area 6 (or lures the troll into the Second Armoury).
   • The Mauler awakens! Iron scrap rattles. Strike Fear test (VALOUR vs Heart TN: Torvir 18,
     Einar 17, Khoril 16).
   • Tactical Options: Riddle duels in Forward stance (RIDDLE [Wits TN]) to confuse the beast,
     firing the repaired ballista, or dropping a ceiling slag-worm onto its iron hide.
   • Midpoint Twist: Grimnar the Disgraced launches an ambush from the catwalks with his Orcs!
   • Torvir and the vanguard engage Grimnar, reclaiming the Stolen Dagger of Durin.

2. THE OPENING OF THE VAULT (30 min):
   • Torvir uses the Blood of Durin ritual while Einar inserts the Marshal's Key.
   • The King's Door swings open. Describe the magnificent golden sanctum of Area 10.
   • Claiming Durin's Axe: The runes blaze with silver fire! Eye Awareness spikes by +4!
     Alert surges to Alert Tier 3 (Drums in the Deep!).

3. THE FIGHTING WITHDRAWAL (45 min):
   • General alarms echo through the Deeps. Drums in the deep begin to pound!
   • The companion Band forms a retreating shield-wall, carrying 600 lbs of wargear and ingots.
   • Dúrmer and Bróga hold the Upper Gatehouse, firing crossbows to cover the climb.
   • Triumphant return up the shafts into Thistlebeard's haven, stepping out into the sunlight of
     Dimrill Dale to lay the weapons of Khazad-dûm at the feet of Lord Balin!
========================================================================================
```
