## Keyed Areas

### 1. The Mustering-Yard (Crossroads of the Deep)
*Classification: Landmark (Obscure)*

> *Ni träder ut på en kolossal underjordisk mönstringsplats huggen direkt ur bergets svarta basalt. Åtta väldiga åttakantiga pelare reser sig tolv meter upp i det välvda dunklet. Stengolvet är sprucket och nött av forna tiders järnskodda stövlar. I mitten står en förfallen stenpaviljong, och brutna spjut och rostiga järnsplitter ligger kringspridda över de spruckna hällarna. Ett kallt, svavelmättat drag viner svagt genom valvgångarna som leder ut i mörkret.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch darkness save for two smouldering coke braziers near the central pavilion (dim, flickering red light up to 20 feet).
* **Atmosphere**: Smells of stale grease, cold soot, and sulfur. Echoes carry easily—every loud noise reverberates across the four flanking corridors.
* **Present Forces**: A sentry squad of **4 Orc Soldiers** and **1 Udûn Sniffer** dozing near the pavilion. They are bored, grumbling about rations and arguing over bone dice.
* **Tactical Features**:
  * *Basalt Pillars*: Provide total cover for stealth approaches.
  * *High Balconies*: 20 feet up, accessible via stone rung ladders along the north wall. Excellent vantage point for ranged combat.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Perimeter Infiltration — STEALTH roll**: The Company can slip around the perimeter pillars to the First Armoury (Area 3) or Broken Hall (Area 4) without alerting the sentries.
  * *Modifiers*: Alert Tier 0 grants **+1d**; massive basalt pillars provide total cover.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: A companion's boot or shield clips an iron brazier stanchion, generating **+1 Noise Point** (advancing the Alert Tracker) and alerting the sniffing hounds.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The Company moves like smoke across the basalt floor; generates **+0 Noise** and grants **+1d** to the lead hero's next test in Location 2 or 3.
    * **66**: Flawless silent infiltration; bypasses the sentries completely and identifies the optimal rear ambush angle.
* **Overlook Sentry Scouting — SCAN roll**: Scouting the sentry post from the high balcony before stepping onto open ground.
  * *Modifiers*: A hero utilizing *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured** (roll two Feat dice, keep the higher result). Having a scout on point grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The sentry post is not spotted before entering open ground; Company loses the surprise round if combat starts.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Discovers the sentries dozing behind Pillar #4, notes their bone-dice distraction, and spots the crude charcoal dispatch slate near their brazier.
    * **66**: Also uncovers a discarded Orc message scroll regarding friction between Malech and Thu the Firespeaker (*"Malech demands more iron from the deep foundries. Let the Fire-priests choke on their smoke"*).
* **Ambush Assault — BATTLE roll**: If the Company attacks from surprise, achieving a decisive tactical strike.
  * *Modifiers*: Attacking from the high balconies or unseen pillars grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The assault is clumsy; sentries raise an immediate alarm shout before being engaged (**+2 Noise Points**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The Company eliminates 2 sentries immediately before they can sound an alarm horn (**+1 Noise Point** total for a 1-round quiet kill).
    * **66**: The entire sentry squad is eliminated in absolute silence before a single cry is uttered (**+0 Noise**).
* **Ancient Scorch Analysis — LORE roll or BATTLE roll**: Inspecting the vitrified scorch marks on the pavilion floor.
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking *Enemy-lore (Orcs)* or *Dwarven Lore* grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Inconclusive inspection; wastes 10 minutes.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Confirms the scorch marks are vitrified Balrog footprints from 1980 T.A. and identifies the ancient engineered Dwarven defensive kill-zones across the plaza.
* **Searching Sentry Camp / Sentry Interrogation — SCAN roll, RIDDLE roll, or AWE roll**: Inspecting the charcoal dispatch slate or interrogating a captured sentry.
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking a relevant Trait such as *Enemy-lore (Orcs)*, *Fierce*, or *Cunning* grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Sentry spits black blood or dies without talking; wastes 5 minutes.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Recovers vital intelligence: confirms **Grimnar the Disgraced** is prowling Level 3C, reveals the **Marshal's Key** was carried down into the deeps, and provides a crude sketch of Location 3's tripwires.
* **Marching Discipline — TRAVEL roll or ENHEARTEN roll**: A Player-Hero leading the Company invokes the *Leadership* Trait (**+1d**) to guide the squad across the open plaza.
  * *Alternative*: Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d) against **Band TN 15**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The squad makes clattering noise (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Complete silence across the 100-pace plaza (**+0 Noise**).

### 2. The Upper Gatehouse (The Shattered Threshold)
*Classification: Fortified Chokepoint*

> *Framför er reser sig de Övre Vapenhallarnas inre porthus, ett monumentalt bålverk av tuktad granit byggt rakt in i urberget. Dess massiva stormportar av adamant hänger utåtböjda på grova tappar, ärrade av forntida sprängkraft. Tunga motviktsblock hänger från takets vindspel, och skottgluggar genomborrar de flankerande stenposteringarna ovanför den sluttande rampen.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch black.
* **Tactical Role**: The premier position to establish the **Expedition Rally Camp**. Leaving 2 to 3 companions here secures the Company’s retreat line, prevents rear ambushes, and adds **+2 Band Readiness** during Act III's *Fighting Withdrawal*.
* **Structural Hazard**: The fractured archway overhead is strained.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Skill Endeavour: Fortifying the Forward Redoubt (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Bracing the buckled adamant blast-doors with cedar balks, iron pitons, and granite blocks.
  * *Resistance*: **3**. Each test represents 10 minutes of disciplined labor.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT roll**, **ATHLETICS roll**, **BATTLE roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Assisting companions with *Smith* or *Mighty* traits grant **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Sledgehammer slips or timber creaks loudly (**+1 Noise Point** per failed attempt); if unfinished after 3 attempts, the barricade is partial (+1 Parry instead of Total Cover).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Contributes 3 points of Resistance (completes the endeavour in a single 10-minute turn!).
  * *Outcome on Success*: Establishes the Gatehouse as a **Fortified Forward Redoubt**. A rearguard garrison stationed here receives **Total Cover (+3 Parry, immune to lower missile fire)** and adds **+2 Band Readiness** to the final Fighting Withdrawal.
* **Rigging the Keystone Winch Trap — CRAFT roll or BATTLE roll**:
  * *Context*: Inspecting the overhead 3-ton cracked granite keystone and rigging its ancient winch cable to a quick-release tripwire.
  * *Modifiers*: **Ill-favoured** if attempted in pitch darkness without lantern light.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The rusted winch jams or slips, dropping iron flakes (**+1 Noise Point**) and requiring a reset.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Rigs a hair-trigger trip-rope. During withdrawal, triggering the trap is a free combat reaction, dropping 3 tons of granite (**30 Damage, Protection 4d vs Injury TN 18**) and permanently sealing the southern rampway with 10 tons of rubble (**+4 Noise Points**).
* **Discovering the Maintenance Flue — EXPLORE roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: A hero investigating with *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Flue entrance behind left turret remains hidden; Company must proceed down the main ramp into Location 3.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Locates the narrow crawlway bypassing Location 3 directly into Location 5.
* **Opening the Armoire Wall-Locker — CRAFT roll or STEALTH roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait (or *Vaultbreaker* gifts) grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Heavy bronze lock mechanism jams solid; forcing it requires iron crowbars (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Silent opening; recovers 24 masterwork arrows (+1 Ranged Damage), 4 iron grappling hooks with 50ft rope, and a flask of *Dwarven Blasting Pitch*.

### 3. The First Armoury (The Stripped Hall)
*Classification: Despoiled Storage*

> *Ni stiger ned i en lång, lågvälvd hall där dubbla rader av vapenställ i sten står plundrade och ödsliga. Tusentals tomma tappar kantar väggarna där stridsutrustning en gång hängde. Golvet är täckt av krossade träsköldar, gnagda ben och spridda högar av orkisk bråte, och kalla luftdrag viskar genom skuggorna.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Total darkness.
* **Atmosphere**: Dry, dusty air. The Orcs ransacked this chamber centuries ago, adapting Dwarven blades to their own jagged, venomous weaponry.
* **Traps**: The Orcs have rigged crude **Scrap-Traps** (counterweighted scythe-blades dripping with black venom) across the main aisle.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Detecting Sinew Tripwires — SCAN roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Investigating with *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured**. Having a scout on point grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The tripwire is missed; lead character steps on the cord, triggering the trap!
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Spots both interlocking tripwire lines, the caltrop field, and the loose floor-flag concealing the arming cache beneath rack #12.
* **Skill Endeavour: Disarming the Scythe Scrap-Trap Network (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Wedging counterweighted scythe pivots and snipping taut sinew cords silently.
  * *Resistance*: **3**.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT roll**, **STEALTH roll**, **SCAN roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait (or *Vaultbreaker*) grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: On a failed roll, the mechanism slips. If a Feat die shows an Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$), the scythe swings down! Lead character faces an attack (4d vs Target TN + Shield; on hit: 14 Damage, Piercing Blow Injury TN 16 coated in Black Venom, **+2 Noise Points**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Entire trap network disarmed silently in a single action (**+0 Noise**).
* **Harvesting Black Orc-Venom — HEALING roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Hands shake; vial shatters or blade pricks skin (hero must make Protection test vs Injury TN 14 or suffer the Weary condition and lose 2 Endurance per hour).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Safely harvests 3 pristine vials of *Black Orc-Venom* (applying to a blade grants **+2 Injury Rating** for 1 combat encounter).
* **Re-Arming Traps for Rearguard — CRAFT roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Traps disabled permanently; cannot be reused.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Re-arms scythe blades facing north; during Act III withdrawal, automatically ambushes the Orc vanguard (delays pursuit by 2 rounds).
* **Scavenging the Concealed Cache — HUNTING roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Nothing usable recovered from the scrap.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Sifting through the debris recovers 4 pristine Dwarven War-Mattocks (Damage 6, Injury 18, Break Shield), 6 Throwing Knives (Damage 3, Injury 14), and 2 intact iron crowbars.

### 4. The Broken Hall (Museum of Fallen Valor)
*Classification: Landmark (Obscure)*

> *Denna vidsträckta minneshall bär spår av forntida storslagenhet, buren av räfflade marmorpelare tolv meter upp i mörkret. Men de magnifika väggrelieferna har brutalt huggits sönder och smetats ned med torkat orkblod och tjära. Mitt i salen reser sig en monstruös, tolv fot hög avgudabild av taggigt svart järn, brända ben och smält slagg, formad som Balrogens bevingade skugga. En kvävande kyla strålar från den mörka metallen.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Darkness, but the grotesque eyes of the Dark Lord idol gleam with sickly bioluminescent cave-fungus.
* **Present Hazard**: The desecration radiates a supernatural Dread aura.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Resisting Spiritual Dread — AWE roll or ENHEARTEN roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Dwarves testing vs Orc/Balrog desecration test **Ill-favoured** if they have an active Flaw.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Hero gains **2 Shadow Points (Dread)** and becomes **Miserable** until taking a Rest.
    * *Curse of Vengeance Flaw*: On a failure, a hero with the *Curse of Vengeance* Flaw gains **1 additional Shadow Point (Dread)** and must spend their next action striking the blasphemous effigy (**+2 Noise Points**, 2 Fatigue).
    * *Dragon-sickness Flaw*: On a failure, a hero with the *Dragon-sickness* Flaw is transfixed by the gold-leaf veins, wasting 10 minutes attempting to pry them free.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Unshakable resolve; completely immune to the room's Dread aura and grants **+1d** to an adjacent ally's test.
    * **66**: Resolute defiance; inspires the Company, clearing the **Miserable** condition from all companions and restoring **+1 Hope**.
* **Deciphering the Royal Cartouche — LORE roll or RIDDLE roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Dwarf of Durin heritage grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Runic cipher remains garbled; key mechanics of the King's Door remain unknown until Location 9.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Translates the master cipher (*"The Vault of the Crown shall yield neither to pick nor hammer, save when the Marshal's Baton turns the left ward and the King's Signet turns the right"*), confirming that the King's Door requires both keys or an extraordinary bypass endeavour.
* **Banish the Gloom — SONG roll or ENHEARTEN roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Voice falters in the oppressive gloom; no effect.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Singing *The Song of Durin* cleanses the oppressive aura, removes **Miserable** from all heroes, and restores **+1 Band Hope**.
* **Skill Endeavour: Controlled Toppling of the Balrog Idol (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Rigging crowbars and blanketing the stone base with canvas to topple the 12-foot iron effigy without alarming the deeps.
  * *Resistance*: **3**.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **ATHLETICS roll**, **CRAFT roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Assisting with *Mighty* grants **+1d**; wrapping in blankets/felt (*Craft*) reduces acoustic impact.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Idol crashes unpadded onto granite flagstones, generating **+3 Noise Points (+1 Alert Tier, +1 Eye Awareness)**.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Idol is lowered gently in absolute silence (**+0 Noise**), restores **+1 Band Hope**, and allows 30 silver pennies (3 Treasure) of gold scrap to be safely recovered.
* **Prying the Idol's Jewels — STEALTH roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The precarious skull tilts, dislodging iron scrap (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Pries the embedded jewel without destabilizing the bone frame, recovering an *Elven Tourmaline* (worth 15 Treasure Points).

### 5. The Second Armoury (The Siege Foundry)
*Classification: Heavy Manufactory*

> *Ni träder in i en väldig verkstadshall fylld av doften från torr ceder, maskinolja och kåda. Massiva timmerställningar reser sig mot taket. Uppställda över golvet står Tredje Djupets belägringsmaskiner: täckta murbräckor på stenspår samt torsionsballistor och motvägda takkranar vars senbuntar bevarats under inoljat läder.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch black.
* **Tactical Utility**: The siege engines provide immense tactical leverage for the Company.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Skill Endeavour: Calibrating & Arming the Siege Engines (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Oiling bronze guide-tracks, stringing preserved sinew skeins, and loading the 6-foot star-iron harpoon.
  * *Resistance*: **3**.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT roll**, **ATHLETICS roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Assisting with *Smith* grants **+1d**; Dwarven engineering tools grant **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Rusted gears grind loudly (**+1 Noise Point** per failed attempt); takes 20 minutes instead of 10.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Both the Grond-ram and Torsion Ballista are calibrated and cocked with masterwork precision.
  * *Mechanical Payoff*:
    * *Grond-Ram*: Released down the bronze tracks into Location 6, dealing **25 Direct Damage** to The Mauler (ignoring armour), knocking it Prone, and stripping 2d from its scrap carapace (**+4 Noise Points**).
    * *Torsion Ballista*: Ranged attack roll 4d vs Target TN (Damage 30, Piercing Blow Injury TN 22, anchors troll to doorway).
* **Designing the Chokepoint Kill-Zone — BATTLE roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Poor tactical angles; siege engines cannot cover flanking archways.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Flawless tactical placement; the companion band gains **+1d** to all Band Clash and ranged rolls while fighting in Location 5.
* **Barricading the Passage — ATHLETICS roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking *Mighty* grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Barricade is flimsy; provides only light cover (+1 Parry).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Assembles interlocking cedar balks in 10 minutes, creating a fortified redoubt (+2 Parry, Half Cover) across the northern entrance.
* **Unlocking the Engineer's Strongbox — CRAFT roll or STEALTH roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait (or *Vaultbreaker*) grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Complex pin-tumbler jams; must be forced open with crowbars (**+1 Noise Point**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Silent opening; recovers 3 masterwork calipers, 2 flasks *Dwarven Lubricant Oil*, *Iron Spanner* (Club: Damage 4, Injury 14, Break Shield), and 4 star-iron harpoon heads.

### 6. The Hall of the Mauler (Lair of the Armoured Troll)
*Classification: Boss Arena / Monster Lair*

> *En kvävande stank av våt päls, ruttna ben och gammal späckolja fyller denna kolossala amfiteater. Smala gångbryggor av järn korsar det övre valvet tolv meter upp. Golvet nedanför är en ödelagd kyrkogård av krossat järnskrot, deformerade sköldar och krossade stenhällar. Från salens dunkla mitt ekar ett djupt, rytmiskt snarkande som får stengolvet att vibrera under era stövlar.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Complete darkness, punctuated by the occasional glint of metal when a light source is exposed.
* **The Creature**: **The Mauler** (Attribute Level 10 Great Cave-troll, Might 2, Hate 10, Armour 5d, Parry —). Wounded at the Battle of Azanulbizar, the creature lives in paranoid terror of pain and surrounds itself with iron.
* **Grimnar's Ambush**: If the Alert Level is 2+, **Grimnar the Disgraced** and 3 Orc trackers lurk along the high stone catwalks above, waiting for the party to engage the troll before attacking from behind!

#### Tactical Encounters & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **The Catwalk Traverse — STEALTH roll**:
  * *Alternative*: Band **MANOEUVRE** (2d) against **Band TN 15**.
  * *Modifiers*: Moving across scrap floor is **Ill-favoured** (-1d); moving along high catwalks is standard. Having a scout lead grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: A loose chain or iron ring clatters down into the nest (**+2 Noise Points**), waking The Mauler and initiating combat at Alert Tier 2!
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Company traverses the entire 40-pace hall in absolute silence (**+0 Noise**), reaching Location 9 undetected.
* **Combat Task: The Riddle Duel (Forward Stance) — RIDDLE roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: The Mauler's *Dull-Witted* Fell Ability makes this test **Favoured**!
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The troll ignores the mockery and swings its massive maul directly at the taunting hero (**Heavy Blow**, +10 Damage).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The Mauler bellows in confusion, swinging at shadows; it loses **1 point of Hate** plus **1 additional Hate per 6 icon rolled**!
    * **Gandalf Rune (G)**: The brute clutches its skull in terror, losing its entire turn; all Company attacks against it this round are Favoured.
* **Severing the Carapace Wires — HUNTING roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Position*: Forward Stance or from elevated Catwalk.
  * *Modifiers*: Called Shot rules apply (must roll at least one 6 icon).
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Blade bounces off iron plates; hero is exposed to counter-attack.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Snips the master copper wire bundle, dropping The Mauler's scrap Armour rating permanently from **5d to 3d**!
* **Dropping the Limestone Stalactite — ATHLETICS roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: Using iron crowbar or precision ranged shot from catwalk.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Stalactite misses, smashing flagstones (**+3 Noise Points, +1 Alert Tier**).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Direct hit: inflicts **20 Direct Damage** (bypassing Armour), knocks The Mauler **Prone**, and strips 1d from its Armour.
* **Overwatch Scouting — SCAN roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Fails to identify the ceiling fault line.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Spots the cracked limestone stalactites and the hidden slag chute chasm edge.

### 7. The Poisoned Halls (Twelfth & Fourteenth Halls)
*Classification: Landmark (Hazardous / Obscure)*

> *En tung, ockragul dimma vilar midjehögt över det nedsänkta golvet och glöder med en svag, ondskefull fosforescens. Luften luktar skarpt av bränt svavel, metallisk koppar och ozon. Närmast ingången framträder forntida dvärgkrigare i full rustning ur ångan, orörliga som förstenade statyer där Skuggans andedräkt överraskade dem.*

#### GM Information
* **Hazard**: **The Breath of the Pit** (Balrog Neurotoxic Gas).
  * *Unprotected Exposure* (raw breathing): Tested *every minute*. Roll **PROTECTION test**, **Ill-favoured**. Failure inflicts 1d6 Endurance loss; rolling an Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$) drops the character to 0 Endurance (inflicting a Wound; if already Wounded, the hero becomes **Dying**).
  * *Protected Exposure* (vinegar-soaked cloth, chewed mountain herbs via Healing check): Tested *once per hour*. Failure inflicts 1d3 Endurance loss; rolling an Eye of Sauron inflicts Severe Poison.
* **The Preserved Host**: Over 40 Dwarven knights lie here. Their gear is pristine.
* **The Preserved Scribe**: Slumped against the council dais sits **Frár son of Frerin, Scribe of the Third Deep**, clutching a sealed lead tube.

#### Interactables & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Skill Endeavour: Assembling Squad Respirator Masks (Resistance 3)**:
  * *Context*: Constructing airtight masks using oiled leather scraps, charcoal granules, crushed sponge, and crystal goggles.
  * *Resistance*: **3**. Each test represents 10 minutes of labor.
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT roll**, **HEALING roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Assisting with *Smith* grants **+1d**; utilizing workshop supplies from Location 5 grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Flawed filter seals (provides only *Protected* status rather than full immunity).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Contributes 2 points of Resistance.
    * **66**: Completes masterwork respirators providing **4 hours of complete gas immunity** for up to 10 companions!
* **Unjamming the Overhead Ventilation Flue — ATHLETICS roll or CRAFT roll**:
  * *Modifiers*: **Ill-favoured** due to rusted iron corrosion. Invoking *Mighty* grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: The rusted iron lever binds solid; hero suffers 2 Fatigue from physical exertion.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Lever frees with a screech (**+3 Noise Points**); exhaust flues open, venting the entire hall clear of toxic gas in 2 combat rounds!

### 9. The King's Door (The Adamant Portal)

#### GM Information
* **The Lock Mechanism**: The door requires both the *King's Key* and the *Marshal's Key* (or runic/craft bypasses) to open smoothly.
* **Ambush Threat**: **Grimnar the Disgraced** (AL 6 Stalker, Might 2, Hate 6, Parry +2) and 4 Udûn Stalkers positioned on overhead parapet 15 feet above.

#### Solutions, Bypasses & TOR 2e Skill Checks
* **Overhead Ambush Detection — AWARENESS roll**:
  * *Alternative*: Band **VIGILANCE** (2d) against **Band TN 15**.
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Wary* Trait grants **+1d**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Grimnar and his stalkers spring their ambush, gaining a **Surprise Round** (+1d to attacks; heroes cannot use Shield Parry).
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: Spots the glint of poisoned javelins in the murder-holes before stepping into the kill-zone; Company avoids surprise and gains a free ranged volley.
* **Skill Endeavour: Bypassing the Adamant Runic Lock (Resistance 6)**:
  * *Context*: Picking the dual meteoric-iron and adamant tumblers if the *Marshal's Key* or royal blood is unavailable.
  * *Resistance*: **6**. Each attempt represents 1 combat round (or 5 minutes outside combat).
  * *Allowed Skills*: **CRAFT roll**, **STEALTH roll**, **RIDDLE roll**.
  * *Modifiers*: Invoking the *Burglary* Trait grants **+1d**; using *The Broken Key* rolls **Favoured**; assistance from a companion with *Vaultbreaker* grants **+1d**.

  * *Outcome on Success*: The twin adamant wards click back in harmonic sequence; the King's Door swings open in total silence (**+0 Noise**).
* **The Blood of Durin Inscription Ritual — AWE roll or ENHEARTEN roll**:
  * *Context*: A Dwarf hero of Durin's royal lineage slices their palm and presses royal blood against the Ithildin Crown rune.
  * *Modifiers*: Heir of Durin lineage grants **+1d** and **Favoured**.
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Hero loses 2 Endurance from the cut; the Crown rune fails to awaken; 1 Shadow Point (Dread) gained.
  * *Degrees of Success (6 icons)*:
    * **6**: The Ithildin Crown blazes with white starlight; the royal ward disengages instantly with a deep resonant tone.
* **Deciphering the Ithildin Invocation — LORE roll or RIDDLE roll**:
  * *Consequence of Failure*: Inscription remains dormant.
