## Keyed Areas

### 1. The Mustering-Yard (Crossroads of the Deep)
*Classification: Landmark (Obscure)*

> *You emerge into a colossal subterranean plaza. Eight massive basalt octagonal pillars rise into the vaulted gloom forty feet above, their carved capitals scarred by ancient, blackened heat. The stone floor is cracked, scored by the passage of long-dead iron boots and blasted by dragon-fire. At the center stands a ruined stone pavilion, now hung with coarse Orc hide curtains and charred braziers. Faint drafts whistle through the four grand archways leading into the dark.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch darkness save for two smouldering coke braziers near the central pavilion (dim, flickering red light up to 20 feet).
* **Atmosphere**: Smells of stale grease, cold soot, and sulfur. Echoes carry easily—every loud noise reverberates across the four flanking corridors.
* **Present Forces**: A sentry squad of **4 Orc Soldiers** and **1 Udûn Sniffer** dozing near the pavilion. They are bored, grumbling about rations and arguing over bone dice.
* **Tactical Features**:
  * *Basalt Pillars*: Provide total cover for stealth approaches.
  * *High Balconies*: 20 feet up, accessible via stone rung ladders along the north wall. Perfect for sniper placement (Bláin and Bróga).

#### Interactables & Skill Checks
* **Stealth Infiltration (STEALTH TN 14)**: The Company can slip around the perimeter pillars to the First Armoury (Area 3) or Broken Hall (Area 4) without alerting the sentries.
* **Ambush Assault (BATTLE TN 14)**: If the party attacks, achieving a surprise round allows Torvir and the Dwarf vanguard to cut down 2 sentries immediately before they can sound an alarm horn.
* **Scan the Pavilion (SCAN TN 14 / Einar with Broken Key rolls with +2)**: Searching the pavilion reveals a discarded Orc message scroll written in crude runes: *"Malech demands more iron from the deep foundries. Let the Fire-priests choke on their smoke."* (Reveals the friction between Malech and the Udûn cult).

### 2. The Upper Gatehouse (The Shattered Threshold)
*Classification: Fortified Chokepoint*

> *A monumental pair of adamant blast-doors once barred entry into the Armouries. Now, both doors hang shattered on colossal iron pintles—one warped outward as if struck by a titanic hammer of flame, the other half-melted into a slag heap of distorted runes. Heavy defensive arrow-slits pierce the flanking stone redoubts, looking down onto the descending approach stair.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch black.
* **Tactical Role**: The premier position to establish the **Expedition Rally Camp**. Leaving 2 Dwarf companions (e.g. *Dúrmer* and *Bróga*) here secures the Company’s retreat line, prevents rear ambushes, and reduces Alert escalation by -1 AP per Act.
* **Structural Hazard**: The fractured archway overhead is strained. 

#### Interactables & Skill Checks
* **Fortify the Gatehouse (CRAFT TN 14)**: *Fáin* and *Hjoldring* can use iron wedges and timbers to brace the shattered door. If reinforced, it will hold against any Orc force for at least 1 hour during an extraction.
* **Spot Overhead Slag-Worm (AWARENESS TN 14)**: A congealed 15-foot slag-worm clings to the archway above. It can be rigged with a tripwire to fall on pursuers (deals 8 damage and blocks the corridor).

### 3. The First Armoury (The Stripped Hall)
*Classification: Despoiled Storage*

> *Endless rows of stone racks and iron shelving stretch into the shadows, stripped bare of their legendary arms. Thousands of empty pegs line the walls where swords, battleaxes, and shields once hung in gleaming ranks. The floor is strewn with rusted barrel hoops, splintered wooden crates, and shattered stone pedestals.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Total darkness.
* **Atmosphere**: Dry, dusty air. The Orcs ransacked this chamber centuries ago, adapting Dwarven blades to their own jagged, venomous weaponry.
* **Traps**: The Orcs have rigged crude **Scrap-Traps** (tripwires attached to hanging clusters of jagged iron blades) across the main aisle.

#### Interactables & Skill Checks
* **Detect Traps (SCAN TN 14 / Einar with Broken Key rolls with +2)**: Spotting the tripwire prevents triggering the falling blade trap. If triggered, the lead hero must test **ATHLETICS** (TN 14) or suffer 4 Damage and make a loud metallic crash (**+2 Alert Points**).
* **Scavenge the Debris (HUNTING / CRAFT TN 14)**: Sifting through the debris recovers **12 usable Dwarf-forged spearheads** and **2 intact iron crowbars** (invaluable for prying open sealed stone chests).

### 4. The Broken Hall (Museum of Fallen Valor)
*Classification: Landmark (Obscure)*

> *This vast, sorrowful gallery was once a memorial museum dedicated to the heroes of Khazad-dûm. Splintered shields, cloven helms, and notched axes once rested on marble plinths before towering murals of ancient victories against Morgoth and the Dragons of the First Age. Now, the murals have been savagely hacked and smeared with black pitch and dried blood. In place of the Dwarven high-altar stands a grotesque 12-foot effigy constructed from rusted iron, jagged bones, and ox skulls—the Orcish idol of the Dark Lord.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Darkness, but the grotesque eyes of the Dark Lord idol gleam with sickly bioluminescent cave-fungus.
* **Shadow Hazard**: The desecration is heart-wrenching. All heroes must make a **VALOUR Test (TN 14)**:
  * *Failure*: Suffer **1 point of Shadow (Dread)**. 
  * *Torvir's Flaw (Curse of Vengeance)*: If Torvir fails, he gains **2 Shadow** and feels an uncontrollable urge to smash the Orc idol, requiring a **HEART / VALOUR** roll to restrain himself from making noise (**+2 AP** if smashed).
  * *Einar's Flaw (Dragon-sickness)*: Einar spots a glint of gold embedded in the idol's skull-eye.

#### Interactables & Skill Checks
* **Decipher the Desecrated Murals (LORE TN 14)**: Beneath the black pitch on the south wall, a hero uncovers an intact carved relief showing King Durin IV inspecting the defenses of the **Last Redoubt** (Old Moria). The inscription reveals: *"The King's Key rests ever with the Crown in the Hall of the Throne, while the Marshal bears the Iron Key to the Deeps."* (Confirms the lore of the two keys).
* **Plunder the Idol's Eye (STEALTH / SLEIGHT TN 14)**: Einar can pry the embedded jewel from the skull without toppling the precarious bone idol. On success, recovers an **Elven Tourmaline worth 15 Treasure Points**.

### 5. The Second Armoury (The Siege Foundry)
*Classification: Heavy Manufactory*

> *Unlike the stripped weapon racks of the First Armoury, this massive vaulted hall was dedicated to heavy war engines. Four colossal Dwarven ballistas and two iron-plated battering rams mounted on stone tracks stand like sleeping behemoths amid heaps of petrified counterweights and iron cables. The Orcs lacked the engineering skill to move these engines, leaving them frozen in time.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Pitch black.
* **Tactical Utility**: The siege engines provide immense tactical leverage for the Company.

#### Interactables & Skill Checks
* **Repair a Dwarf Ballista (CRAFT TN 14 / Hjoldring assists)**: *Fáin* and *Hjoldring* can re-string and prime one heavy ballista within 15 minutes. It can be fired down the main hall at **The Mauler** or an Orc swarm, dealing **24 Damage (Automatic Piercing Blow)** to any colossal target!
* **Barricade the Passage (ATHLETICS TN 14)**: Dwarves can lever an iron battering ram across the southern archway, blocking Orc reinforcements from flanking them during Act III.

### 6. The Hall of the Mauler (Lair of the Armoured Troll)
*Classification: Boss Arena / Monster Lair*

> *A foul, suffocating stench of wet fur, rotting bone, and troll dung fills this vaulted amphitheater. Heaps of rusted mail-shirts, bent breastplates, and iron greaves are piled ten feet high around the perimeter like grotesque metal nests. At the center of the hall, resting on a bed of smashed shields, sleeps The Mauler—a Great Cave-troll of terrifying proportions, its entire body encased in an absurd, overlapping carapace of iron plates, chain sheets, and Dwarven shields bolted directly into its hide.*

#### GM Information
* **Lighting**: Complete darkness, punctuated by the occasional glint of metal when a light source is exposed.
* **The Creature**: **The Mauler** (Attribute Level 10 Great Cave-troll). Wounded at the Battle of Azanulbizar, the creature lives in paranoid terror of pain and surrounds itself with iron.
* **Stealth Challenge**: Crossing the chamber to the King's Door requires walking across a floor strewn with loose iron rings and metal scrap (**STEALTH TN 16**).
* **Grimnar's Ambush**: If the Alert Level is 2+, **Grimnar the Disgraced** and 3 Orc trackers lurk along the high stone catwalks above, waiting for the party to engage the troll before attacking from behind!

#### Tactical Encounters & Solutions
* **The Riddle Combat Task (FORWARD STANCE / RIDDLE TN 14)**: A hero (e.g. Einar or Khoril) can taunt and confuse the dull-witted beast with echoes and riddles, causing The Mauler to lose **1 Hate per success icon** as it bellows in confusion and attacks empty air.
* **Environmental Takedown**: Luring the troll beneath the cracked ceiling arch (spotted via **SCAN TN 12**) and hitting the support pillar causes 5 tons of stone to collapse onto the beast (inflicts **30 Damage** and pins it for 3 rounds).

### 7. The Poisoned Halls (Twelfth & Fourteenth Halls)
*Classification: Landmark (Hazardous / Obscure)*

> *A heavy, ochre mist hangs waist-deep over the polished stone floor, glowing with a faint, malevolent phosphorescence. The air smells sharply of burnt sulfur, copper, and dead stars. Across the hall lie dozens of Dwarven warriors in magnificent suits of warded mail, their bearded faces resting against their battleaxes as if asleep in death. Preserved by the very miasma that choked their lungs a thousand years ago, the Tunnel-guards remain unbroken.*

#### GM Information
* **Hazard**: **The Breath of the Pit** (Balrog Neurotoxic Gas). Exposure without field precautions requires Endurance tests every minute. With protective masks/herbs, test once per hour (TN 14).
* **The Preserved Host**: Over 40 Dwarven knights lie here. Their gear is pristine, but looting their personal remains directly tests the Dwarves' honor (**VALOUR TN 12** to take wargear respectfully with a prayer of reclaim).

#### Interactables & Clues
* **The Scribe's Dying Letter (SCAN TN 14 / Einar with Broken Key)**: In the hands of a preserved Dwarven officer near the central dais rests a vellum letter, preserved by the gas:
  > *"The Balrog's breath fills the shafts... The Marshal has fallen in the Sixteenth Hall of the Fifth Deep, and his Iron Key was carried down into the warrens by the goblin-filth. Let the King send his key from the Redoubt, or the lower vault is sealed forever..."*
* **Looting Masterwork Armor**: The party can recover **3 complete suits of Dwarf-forged Heavy Mail** and **2 Moria-silver Shields** from the fallen knights.

### 8. The Upper Armoury (The Gas-Filled Cache)
*Classification: Vault Cache*

> *This sealed vault sits directly behind the Poisoned Halls, inundated with thick sulfurous vapour. Near the entryway lie the withered skeletons of a dozen Goblins who attempted to breach the room centuries ago and perished on the threshold. Beyond them stand iron racks holding untouched Dwarven wargear.*

#### GM Information
* **Gas Density**: Heavy. Precautions must be maintained.
* **Salvage Value**: Contains **30 pristine Dwarven Mattocks and Broadswords**, enough to fully equip Balin's entire vanguard at the East-Gate Camp!

### 9. The King's Door (The Adamant Portal)
*Classification: Puzzle / Barrier*

> *At the deepest end of the complex stands The King's Door—a monolithic slab of seamless black adamant inlaid with pure Ithildin. The inlay depicts the Crown-and-Anvil of Durin, flanked by two towering Dwarven warriors with axes raised. In the center of the door are two distinct keyholes: one forged of lustrous Mithril-alloy (The King's Keyhole), the other shaped from dark meteoric iron (The Marshal's Keyhole).*

#### GM Information
* **The Lock Mechanism**: The door requires both the *King's Key* and the *Marshal's Key* to open smoothly.

#### Solutions & Bypasses
1. **The Marshal's Key**:
   * *Path A*: Recovered from the Udûn Sniffer commander or bargained from **Grik the Goblin** in the connecting shaft for 10 Treasure Points or a promise of safety.
   * *Path B*: Picked by Einar using *The Broken Key* and fine Dwarven smithing tools (**Extended CRAFT Endeavour: Resistance 6, TN 16**).
2. **The King's Key**:
   * *Lore Solution*: The King's Key is lost in the Last Redoubt (Chapter 5, p. 92). However, an inscription above the door in Khuzdul runes reads: *"Where the King walks not, the blood of Durin and the craft of Aulë may yet command the stone."*
   * *The Blood of Durin Ritual*: Torvir or Khoril (being of Durin's royal line) can slice their palm and press their blood against the Crown rune while making a **VALOUR TN 14** test. On success, the royal lock clicks open with a deep resonance!

### 10. The Lower Armoury (The Royal Treasury Vault)
*Classification: Landmark (Hidden / Legendary Hoard)*

> *The heavy adamant doors swing inward in breathless silence. Before you opens a pristine, vaulted sanctum of polished granite and gold inlay. Air ancient and pure rushes past your face. Along the walls, upon pedestals of carved crystal, rest the greatest martial masterpieces of Khazad-dûm. At the center of the vault, resting upon an anvil of pure dark iron, rests a massive great axe inlaid with blazing silver runes that pulse with a faint, warm light.*

#### GM Information
* **The Prize**: **Durin's Axe** (The legendary royal weapon forged in the Elder Days).
* **The Greater Hoard**: Contains **120 Treasure Points** in ancient gold ingots, 4 Famous Weapons/Armour (see `04_loot_relics_and_rewards.md`), and cases of First Age Mithril-alloy wargear.
* **The Eye Awakens**: Taking *Durin's Axe* from the anvil causes an immediate subterranean resonance through the mountain: **Eye Awareness increases by +4 immediately!**
