# Session Prep: Balin's Arrival & The Extended Fellowship Phase
**System**: *The One Ring 2e* / *Moria: Through the Doors of Durin*  
**Scope**: Journey to the Safe Haven, Camp Interludes, Balin's Council, and Fellowship Phase Undertakings

---

## Part 1: Resolving the Ascent to the Safe Haven

### 1.1 The Return Journey Roll
* **Context**: The Company ascends from Thrym Thistlebeard's Caves back to the First Hall / East-Gate Camp, escorting 6 companions and 3 rescued captives (*Frór, The Traumatized Dwarf, Haldor*).
* **Mechanics**: Khoril leads as Guide with a single **Travel** roll (TN 14).
  * **Success**: The party navigates the climbing shafts without incident, stepping into the cool drafts of the Second Hall and out into the sunlight of Dimrill Dale.
  * **Failure**: Introduce a single **Journey Event** from the *Solo Journey Events Table* (p. 220–221).
    * *Example: Watchful Eyes (p. 221)*: Udûn sniffers pick up the scent near the ventilation shafts. Test **AWARENESS** / **VIGILANCE** to slip past without adding to Fatigue or Dread.

## Part 2: The Extended Fellowship Phase at Dimrill Dale

### 2.1 Character & Band Recovery (Extended Duration)
* **Full Endurance & Hope**:
  * **Einar**: Restores Hope from **4 to 11**.
  * **Torvir**: Restores Hope from **7 to 10**.
  * **Khoril**: Restores Hope from **9 to 11**.
* **Spiritual Recovery (Shadow Cleansing)**:
  * By raiding the Udûn slave-mines and freeing innocent captives, the party actively hindered the Enemy. Each hero removes **2 Shadow Points**:
    * *Einar*: 4 → 2 Shadow.
    * *Torvir*: 2 → 0 Shadow.
    * *Khoril*: 3 → 1 Shadow.
* **Band Healing**:
  * Clear all injuries and fatigue on **Dúrmer**, **Bróga**, and **Austri**.
* **Ally Promotion**:
  * Select 1 surviving companion (e.g. *Dúrmer* or *Hjoldring*) to become **Hardened** (permanently boosting Band Readiness).

### 2.2 Fellowship Interruption Check (p. 224)
* **Rule**: Roll a Feat Die before Undertakings.
  * *Year 1 Modifier*: Favoured (roll 2 Feat dice, keep the better result).
  * *Extended Duration Modifier*: Ill-favoured (cancels out to a standard single Feat Die roll).
* **On an Eye of Sauron ($\oint$)**: A crisis strikes, costing 1 Undertaking!
  * Roll on the **Interruption Table** (p. 224) to see what crisis strikes the camp:
    * **$\oint$**: Orcs attack in force!
    * **1**: Madness among Balin’s folk
    * **2**: Orcs test your defences
    * **3**: Drums in the deep
    * **4**: Supplies run short
    * **5**: Saboteurs or assassins strike
    * **6**: Orcs encamp nearby
    * **7**: Strife among Balin’s folk
    * **8**: Collapsing or unstable environs
    * **9**: Squabbles among your Band
    * **10**: Goblin spies skulking about
    * **Gandalf Rune**: Dark dreams plague your rest

### 2.3 Undertaking: *Reclaim a Safe Haven (Caves of Thrym Thistlebeard)*
* **Rule (p. 224–225)**: No roll required. By committing time during this Extended Fellowship Phase:
  * Thistlebeard's Caves become an established Dwarven Safe Haven.
  * The path between the First Hall and Thistlebeard shifts from **Dark Land** to **Wild Land**.
  * Each hero receives **3 Adventure Points** and **3 Skill Points** (Experience Milestone).

## Part 3: Balin's Arrival & The Council of Dimrill Dale

### 3.1 The Scene: Lord Balin Steps Forth
Balin son of Fundin arrives in Dimrill Dale with fresh pack-ponies, barrels of iron rations, and a guard of veteran Longbeards. Standing before the East-Gate, he embraces the three heroes:

> *"You have forced the East-Gate, held Durin's Bridge, and pierced the dark to Thistlebeard’s halls. When Fróra’s ravens reached Erebor, I could not remain behind. Yet fifty Dwarves cannot hold a realm carved for legions. King Dáin must be brought to see the truth."*

### 3.2 The Political Dilemma: Convincing King Dáin
Balin lays out two potential courses of action regarding King Dáin Ironfoot:

1. **The Emissary with the "Tribute of Durin"**:
   * Balin sends an elder herald or Fróra back across Wilderland to Erebor, bearing the party's recovered relics (*The Eye of Thrym, The Broken Key, The Beleriand Necklace, Thistlebeard's ancient tomes*) to lay at Dáin's throne as undeniable proof of Moria's reclamation.
   * *Party Choice*: The 3 PCs remain in Moria to prepare for their next deep delve before the Orc factions counterattack.
   * *RP Friction*: Einar (Dragon-sickness) or Torvir may hesitate to surrender their hard-won magical relics to a distant king.
2. **The Heroes Ride to Erebor (The Royal Embassy)**:
   * The party undertakes the long overland journey to Erebor to conduct a formal **Council** before King Dáin.
   * *Stakes*: Leaves Moria under Balin's small garrison for months, risking Orc counter-offensives against the Bridge.

### 3.3 Camp Roleplay Hooks
* **Frór (Bildor's Brother)**: Sits beside the forge. He confirms Bildor's long-standing corruption and warns that if Bildor survived the fight in the mine, he knows every hidden crevice between Thistlebeard and the Deeps.
* **The Traumatized Dwarf**: Sketches charcoal outlines of ancient dwarven architecture on the flagstones, mumbling about old armouries and suits of gromril-mail.
* **Haldor the Human**: Pledges his labour to maintain the camp fortifications, expressing deep gratitude to his Dwarven saviours.
