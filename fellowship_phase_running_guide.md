# FELLOWSHIP PHASE RUNNING GUIDE (MORIA & STRIDER RULES)

> *"There was a great council held in the chamber that evening... Balin sat upon a stone throne, and all his folk were gathered round him, listening to the deeds of the vanguard."*  
> — *The Book of Mazarbul*

**System**: *The One Ring 2e* / *Moria: Through the Doors of Durin* / *Strider Mode*  
**Scope**: Complete procedure for running Fellowship Phases during subterranean expeditions, resolving recovery, downtime interruptions, experience advancement, band management, undertakings, and mission preparation.

---

## Step 1: Set Duration & Safe Haven

Select an established resting location (e.g., *First Hall Encampment, Twenty-First Hall, East-Gate Camp, or a Reclaimed Forward Redoubt like Thrym Thistlebeard's Caves*) and determine the downtime duration.

*(Note: Balin’s expedition in Moria does not observe peaceful seasonal Yule downtime. Underground rests are tactical, operational interludes.)*

* **Hurried (Hours)**: Emergency turnaround and rapid patch-up. **0 Undertakings**.
* **Brief (Days)**: Short rest, triage, resupply, and tactical planning. **1 Undertaking**.
* **Extended (Weeks)**: Full recuperation, crafting, fortification, and major projects. **2 Undertakings**.

---

## Step 2: Physical, Mental & Band Recovery

Resolve all spiritual, physical, and squad recovery based on the chosen rest duration:

| Track / Character Stat | Hurried (Hours) | Brief (Days) | Extended (Weeks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero Endurance** | Full recovery | Full recovery | Full recovery |
| **Hero Hope** | None | Recover = **HEART** score | Full recovery to Maximum |
| **Spiritual Recovery (Shadow)** | None | Remove **1–3 Shadow** *(self-assessed)* | Remove **1–3 Shadow** *(self-assessed)* |
| **Hero Wounds** | Persists | Cleared *(unless untreated/Dying)* | Cleared |
| **Band Fatigue** | Persists | Clear *Fatigued* & *Faltering* | Clear all Fatigue conditions |
| **Band Injuries** | Clear *Fleeting* only | Clear *Fleeting* & *Moderate* | Clear all Injuries *(Severe, Grievous, Lingering)* |
| **Band Wasted Gifts** | Persists | Reset all Wasted Gifts | Reset all Wasted Gifts |
| **Band Hope & Morale** | None | Restores up to **Half Pool (6)** | Restores to **Full Pool (12)** |

### Solo / Strider Spiritual Recovery Guidelines (Shadow Removal)
Evaluate deeds accomplished during the preceding delve:
* **1 Point**: Actions marginally hindered enemy scouts, averted minor traps, or secured basic survival.
* **2 Points**: Deeds actively damaged or set back the Enemy (e.g., cleared a major garrison choke point, defeated an Orc chieftain, liberated slave-mine captives).
* **3 Points**: A monumental blow struck against the Shadow (e.g., reclaimed an ancient Great Hall, destroyed a Balrog/Morgoth shrine, retrieved an iconic dwarven relic).

## Step 3: Fellowship Interruption Check (Moria)

If taking a **Brief** or **Extended** phase, roll **1 Feat Die** before updates and undertakings:

### Modifiers:
* **Phase Duration**: *Brief Phase*: Standard roll; *Extended Phase*: Roll is **Ill-favoured** (roll twice, take the worse result).
* **Campaign Year**: *Year 1 (2989 TA)*: Roll is **Favoured**; *Years 2–3*: Standard; *Year 4+ (2992+ TA)*: Roll is **Ill-favoured**.  
  *(Note: Favoured and Ill-favoured cancel each other out to a standard single die roll).*

### Results:
* **Gandalf Rune ($\mathbf{G}$)**: **Expedition Boon / Breakthrough**. Gain $+1$ Free Fellowship Point, $+1$ temporary Band Readiness on the next mission, or fresh reinforcements/supplies arrive from Erebor.
* **Numerical (1–10)**: **Peace in Camp**. Downtime proceeds cleanly without interference.
* **Eye of Sauron ($\mathbf{S}$)**: **Camp Crisis!** You lose **1 Undertaking** due to addressing the crisis immediately. Roll a Feat Die on the **Moria Interruption Table** below to determine the crisis:

```
 FEAT DIE   CRISIS EVENT (EYE OF SAURON RESULT)
 ─────────  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    1       Madness among Balin's Folk: Paranoia spreads; Band loses 2 Hope.
    2       Orcs Test the Defences: Skirmish at the gate; 1 Ally suffers Fleeting Injury.
    3       Drums in the Deep: Ambient Dread rises; Hunt Threshold -1 on next mission.
    4       Supplies Run Short: Spoiled rations; Heroes gain 1 Fatigue point.
    5       Saboteurs or Assassins Strike: Sentry attacked; Useful Item damaged.
    6       Orcs Encamp Nearby: Starting Eye Awareness +2 on next mission.
    7       Strife among Balin's Folk: Political tension; Fellowship Pool reduced by 1.
    8       Collapsing / Unstable Environs: Barricade collapses; labor required to clear.
    9       Squabbles among your Band: Discipline slips; Band Readiness -1 for 1 delve.
   10       Goblin Spies Skulking About: Infiltration attempt; test Vigilance to catch them.
    S       Orcs Attack in Force! Pitch battle at the redoubt; all heroes make Battle test.
    G       Dark Dreams Plague Your Rest: Premonitions of Durin's Bane; gain 1 Shadow.
```

## Step 4: Perform Character & Band Updates

### 1. Experience Costs Table

| Target Rank / Level | Skill Points (Common Skills) | Adventure Points (Combat Proficiencies, Valour, Wisdom) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Rank 1** | 4 | 4 |
| **Rank 2** | 8 | 8 |
| **Rank 3** | 12 | 12 |
| **Rank 4** | 20 | 20 |
| **Rank 5** | 26 | 26 |
| **Rank 6** | 30 | 30 |

* **Skill Points**: Raise common skill ranks (maximum $+1$ rank per individual Skill per phase).
* **Adventure Points**: Raise Combat Proficiencies (maximum $+1$ rank per proficiency), OR gain 1 rank in **Valour** OR **Wisdom** (cannot raise both in the same phase).
  * **New Valour Rank**: Grants a **Reward** (or awakens a dormant quality on a Famous/Cultural weapon).
  * **New Wisdom Rank**: Grants a **Virtue**.
* **Swap Useful Items**: Change or swap your Player-Hero's Useful Items within Standard of Living limits.

### 2. Band Improvement & Roster Hardening
* **Hardened Status**: Select **1 surviving Ally** who distinguished themselves on the last mission and mark them as **Hardened**. Each Hardened Ally deployed on a mission provides **$+1$ to Band Readiness** (reducing the Band TN by 1).
* **Mitigate Band Trauma**: Spend **1 Fellowship Point** to remove **1 point of Band Shadow**.

## Step 5: Choose Undertakings

Select undertakings up to your allowed duration limit (**0 for Hurried, 1 for Brief, 2 for Extended**).

### Calling Bonuses (1 Free Specific Undertaking per Phase):
* **Captain**: *Strengthen Fellowship* is **Free**.
* **Champion**: *Heal Scars* or *Train Combat Prowess* costs **0 Undertaking slots** (still pay AP costs).
* **Treasure Hunter**: *Visit the Treasury & Forge* or *Appraise Relics* is **Free**.
* **Scholar**: *Study the Records of Mazarbul* or *Decipher Stone Lore* is **Free**.
* **Messenger**: *Meet Patron (Balin / Fróra / Mjolin)* is **Free**.
* **Warden / Slayer**: *Fortify Redoubt / Guard Perimeter* is **Free**.

### Available Undertakings:

1. **Recruit Allies (Moria)**: Expand and replenish the Band roster. If fewer than 4 allies remain, recruit up to 4. Otherwise, recruit 1 new Dwarven Ally per rank in **Valour**.
2. **Reclaim a Safe Haven (Moria — Extended Only)**: Fortify and consecrate a reclaimed landmark (e.g. *Thrym Thistlebeard's Caves*). The travel path connecting this haven to Balin's main camp shifts from **Dark Land** to **Wild Land**. Each participating hero gains **3 Adventure Points and 3 Skill Points**.
3. **Study the Records of Mazarbul**: Research ancient maps, blueprints, and records of the First Age. Gain **$+2$ to Feat die rolls** on Subterranean Journey Events until the next Fellowship Phase.
4. **Meet Patron (Balin / Fróra / Mjolin)**: Commune for strategic council, report vital reconnaissance, or secure dwarven munitions. Gain $+1\text{d}$ on a designated group test during the next delve or gain a temporary expedition boon.
5. **Visit the Treasury & Forge**: Exchange mundane gear Rewards to unlock dormant enchanted qualities on a Famous Weapon/Armour, or repair shattered dwarven mail-shirts.
6. **Fortify Redoubt / Choke Point**: Reinforce stone gates, set iron portcullises, and construct murder-holes. Reduces Starting Eye Awareness by **$-2$** or grants $+1\text{d}$ to rear-guard tactical checks.
7. **Strengthen Fellowship**: Restore bonds of kinship and morale. Increase Fellowship score by **$+1$** until the next Fellowship Phase.
8. **Heal Scars**: Spend **5 Adventure Points** to remove 1 permanent Shadow Scar.

## Step 6: Setup for a New Mission into Moria

Complete this **6-point operational checklist** before stepping out into the deep dark:

```
========================================================================================
                             MISSION PREPARATION CHECKLIST
========================================================================================
 [ ] 1. Define Mission Objective
        • Primary Goal: (e.g., Scouting, Reclaiming an Armory, Slaying an Archfoe, Escort)
        • Secondary Objectives: (e.g., Salvage Dwarven Mail, Map Ventilation Shafts)

 [ ] 2. Select Band Roster & Calculate Readiness
        • Select active Dwarven Companions (Track any persisting injuries/fatigue)
        • Count deployed Hardened Allies:
            Band Readiness = Base 4 + (Hardened Allies deployed)
            Band Readiness TN = 20 - Band Readiness

 [ ] 3. Assign Tactical Squad Deployments
        • Forward Scout Screen: (e.g., Austri & Bróga — Rolls Vigilance 2d)
        • Shield-Wall Phalanx: (e.g., Dúrmer, Dolg, Bláin — Rolls War 3d)
        • Rearguard Choke Point: (e.g., Bláin & Fáin — Gate defense / escape line)
        • Heavy Salvage Porters: (e.g., Hjoldring & Dúrmer — Carrying capacity)

 [ ] 4. Determine Band Burden & Alert Baseline
        • Starting Burden: Light / Medium / Heavy (Affects marching noise & fatigue)
        • Reset Alert Tracker: Alert 0 (Quiet Shadows, 0–3 Noise Points)

 [ ] 5. Set Starting Eye Awareness
        • Reset base score to 0, then apply modifiers:
            - Small Band (1–4 Allies): +0
            - Medium Band (5–8 Allies): +2
            - Large Band (9–12 Allies): +4
            - Famous Weapons / Radiant Relics: +1 each

 [ ] 6. Calculate Hunt Threshold & Journey Ingress
        • Base Moria Threshold: 14 (Dark Land)
        • Delve Modifiers:
            - Previous Astounding Success: +4 | Qualified: +0 | Devastating: -4
            - Rest Duration Taken: Hurried (+2) | Brief (+0) | Extended (-2)
        • Set marching order and Guide role, then make the initial Marching Test.
========================================================================================
```
